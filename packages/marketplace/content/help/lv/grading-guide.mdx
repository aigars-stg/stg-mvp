Grade honestly. Sell faster. Keep everyone happy.

When you grade your game accurately, buyers know exactly what they are getting. No surprises, no disputes, no disappointment. A well-graded listing sells faster and keeps everyone happy.

<PlainTermsBox>
We use four grades: **Like New**, **Very Good**, **Good**, and **Acceptable**. If you are unsure between two grades, pick the lower one. A buyer who receives a game in better condition than expected is delighted. A buyer who receives worse opens a dispute.
</PlainTermsBox>

## Kā jauns

**Izskatās nespēlēts vai spēlēts vienu reizi** — 80-95% no mazumtirdzniecības vērtības

Opened but essentially untouched. This game looks like you just removed the shrink wrap. Many games get opened, admired, and put on the shelf — if that is your game, this is probably the right grade.

**Kastīte:**

* Nav redzama nodiluma, neskaidrības vai skrapju
* Stūri asas un veselas
* Nav plaukta nodiluma vai glabāšanas iezīmēm
* Sākotnējie ievietošanas un iepakošanas produkti iekļauti

**Komponenti:**

* Visi komponenti pieejami un saņemti
* Kartes nav nodiluma — malas asas, bez poļošanas
* Marķieri neizbaudīti vai nozīmīti bez bojājumiem
* Noteikšanas grāmata skaista, bez krumplīšiem vai iezīmēm
* Dēļi plakani bez saplēšanas

**Typical scenarios:** Opened to read the rules but never played. Received as a gift but not their type of game. Shrink removed to check contents, never set up.

<PlainTermsBox>
If you have played even once or twice, consider Very Good instead. Like New means essentially untouched.
</PlainTermsBox>

## Ļoti labs

**Spēlēts ar aprūpi, minimāls nodilums** — 60-75% no mazumtirdzniecības vērtības

Played and enjoyed, but handled with care. A game in Very Good condition is what you would happily give as a gift. The "gift test": would you wrap this and give it to a friend without apologising for its condition?

**Kastīte:**

* Mazs plaukta nodilums vai viegla neskaidrība
* Stūriem var parādīties neliela mīkstināšana
* Iespējami nelieli virsmas skrapji
* Nav plīsumu, skaļu vai strukturālu bojājumu
* Sākotnējie ievietošanas parasti bijuši

**Komponenti:**

* Visi komponenti pieejami
* Kartes parāda minimālo apstrādi — gaisma mala nodilums vismaksimālā
* Marķieri nozīmīti skaidri, bez pacelšanas vai plīsumiem
* Noteikšanas grāmatica var būt mazos krumplīšos no lietošanas
* Dēļi plakani vai tikai ar minimālu saplēšanu

**Typical scenarios:** Played 3-5 times at careful game nights. Well-maintained in a collection. Cards sleeved from first play. Stored properly on a shelf.

<PlainTermsBox>
This is the sweet spot for most pre-loved games. Buyers get a great game at a fair price.
</PlainTermsBox>

## Labs

**Regulāras spēles nodilums, pilnībā funkcionāls** — 40-55% no mazumtirdzniecības vērtības

Clearly pre-loved — this game has seen regular play but remains fully functional. All the adventures, some of the wear. A game in Good condition plays perfectly fine; the wear is cosmetic, not functional.

**Kastīte:**

* Jūtams nodilums, neskaidrības vai skrapji
* Stūru nodilums — mīksti stūri, iespējams mazos plīsumi
* Iespējami nelieli iespiedumi vai vieta
* Viegls gredzena nodilums vai uzlīmes atlikumi
* Sākotnējie ievietošanas var trūkt vai bojāts

**Komponenti:**

* Visi komponenti pieejami (vai trūkstošie gabali skaidri norādīti)
* Kartes parāda jaukšanas nodilumu malās
* Dažiem komponentiem var būt spēles nodilums
* Noteikšanas grāmatica var būt krumplīši, mazos plīsumi vai piezīmes
* Dēļi var būt neliels saplēšana vai mala nodilums

**Typical scenarios:** Regular game night favourite. Passed between friends. Well-loved family game. Cafe or club copy.

<PlainTermsBox>
Many collectors are happy with Good condition for games they want to play rather than display.
</PlainTermsBox>

## Pieņemams

**Smagas nodiluma, joprojām spēlējamas** — 20-35% no mazumtirdzniecības vērtības

Well-loved and showing it. This game works, but it has seen better days. Always note what is missing or damaged in your listing description — buyers deserve to know exactly what they are getting.

**Kastīte:**

* Nozīmīgs nodilums, krumplīši vai bojājumi
* Stūru plīsumi, plīsumi vai lentes remonti
* Galvenie skrapji, traipa vai rakstīšana
* Kastīte var neatvērt pareizi
* Sākotnējie ievietošanas iespējams trūkst

**Komponenti:**

* Visi svarīgie komponenti pieejami (nelieli gabali var trūkt)
* Kartes nolietoti — var būt nepieciešamas vienjoslas komfortabli spēlēt
* Dažiem marķieriem var būt nodilums, pacēlums vai neliels bojājums
* Noteikšanas grāmatica smagi nolietota, iespējams aizstāta ar drukātu kopiju
* Dēļi var būt jūtams nodilums vai saplēšana
* Var būt aizstājoši komponenti

**Typical scenarios:** Heavily played family favourite. Rescued from a thrift store. Missing a few minor components. Box damaged but game complete.

<PlainTermsBox>
Be very specific about damage. "Acceptable with taped corner" is better than vague descriptions.
</PlainTermsBox>

## Speciālas izskatīšanas

### Nozīmīti vs neizbaudīti marķieri

Vai kartona marķieri ir nozīmīti, jums svarīgi dažiem pircējiem.

* **Neizbaudīti:** marķieri joprojām sākotnējās lapās, nekad noņemti
* **Nozīmīti:** noņemti no lapām (standarts jebkurai spēlētai spēlei)
* **Skaidri nozīmīti:** noņemti uzmanīgi bez pacēluma, plīsuma vai bojājuma
* **Rupji nozīmīti:** daži pacēlumi, plīsumi vai balti malas redzams

### Vienjoslās kartes

Cards protected in sleeves are a plus. Note this in your listing.

* Kartes vienjoslas no pirkuma (nekad jauktas bez vienjoslā)
* Kartes vienjoslas pēc dažas spēles (pierakstiet jebkuru pirms vienjoslā nodiluma)
* Piezīmē, ja vienjoslas iekļautas vai ja kartes tiks noņemtas

### Krāsotu miniatūras

Some buyers want painted minis, others specifically do not. Be clear about the state: unpainted (original), primed only, partially painted, or fully painted (describe quality).

### Promos un papildinājumi

Promotional content, Kickstarter extras, or expansion material adds value — make sure buyers know. List specific promos by name and note if from Kickstarter or retail edition.

### Izdevuma un valoda

Edition matters. Different editions can have different components, art, or rules. Always note which edition, publisher, and language of the game.

## Bieži kļūdas

| Kļūda                                           | Labāks pieeja                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Tas ir vecs, tāpēc tam ir jābūt nolietotam"    | Age does not equal condition. A 20-year-old game stored well can be Like New. |
| "Kastīte nav svarīga"                           | It matters to collectors. Many buyers care about box condition for display.   |
| "Pilnīga nozīmē neko nav pazudis"               | Complete also means original. Note any replaced components.                   |
| Izgudrojošas pakāpes, piemēram "Tuvu Mints"     | Stick to our four grades. Custom terms make comparison harder.                |
| Novērtējums pamatojoties uz to, ko jūs maksājāt | Novērtējiet faktisko stāvokli, nevis vērtību.                                 |

## Sludinājuma apraksta piemēri

Not sure what to write? Here are example descriptions for each grade:

**Like New:** "Opened to read the rules, never played. All components still in original packaging. Cards never shuffled. Stored flat on a shelf in a smoke-free home."

**Very Good:** "Played maybe five times. Cards sleeved from first play, no wear. All components present. Original insert intact. Box shows minor shelf wear."

**Good:** "Box shows shelf wear and one soft corner. Inside is in great shape — cards show light edge wear from shuffling. All components present."

**Acceptable:** "Box has significant wear and a taped corner. Components are all there and play fine, but cards show shuffling wear. Rulebook has some creases. Good candidate for sleeving."
