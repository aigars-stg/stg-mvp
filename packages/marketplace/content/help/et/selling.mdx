## 1. Create Your Listing

Go to **Sell a game** and search for your game. We automatically pull the game details from BoardGameGeek. Select the correct edition/language and note any included expansions.

## 2. Grade the Condition

Accurate grading ensures fast sales and prevents buyer disputes. Use our four grades:

* **Like New:** Opened but unplayed. No visible wear. Complete, fully functional.
* **Very Good:** Played with care. Minimal wear. Complete, fully functional.
* **Good:** Regular play. Normal cosmetic wear. Complete, fully functional.
* **Acceptable:** Significant wear or missing components. (You *must* describe exactly what is missing or damaged).

*Golden Rule: If you are unsure between two grades, pick the lower one.*

## 3. Add Photos

Photos are required for Acceptable condition games and highly recommended for all others. Include shots of the box front, box back, components spread out, and close-ups of any specific wear or damage.

## 4. Set Your Price & Method

Choose a fixed price or set up an auction. You can use our pricing assistant to see recent sales data. Next, select your selling method - **Claim this game** or **Message seller**.

## 5. Fulfill Your Order (Claim this game)

When your game sells:

1. **Confirm** the order in your dashboard within **24 hours**.
2. **Pack** the game securely (see our Shipping guide).
3. **Ship** the game within **2 business days** at your nearest parcel terminal, using the code we provide.

Your earnings will be credited to your platform wallet 2 days after the buyer receives the game.
