## The Short Version

**No labels. No post office. Just a code and a locker.**

Shipping on Second Turn Games is simple: we give you a code, you pack your game, you go to a parcel terminal, enter the code, and drop it in. The network handles the rest.

:::plain-terms
The 5-step process: (1) Confirm the order within 24 hours, (2) Receive your shipping code, (3) Pack the game securely, (4) Go to any terminal, enter code, load package, (5) Done — tracking is automatic.
:::

## Step-by-Step: How to Ship

### Step 1: Confirm the Order

When someone buys your game, you will receive a notification (email and in-app). You have **24 hours** to confirm you can ship it.

**To confirm:**
1. Go to your Seller Dashboard → Orders
2. Find the new order
3. Click "Confirm Order"

If you cannot fulfill the order, click "Reject" — the buyer gets a full refund and no penalty applies (occasional rejections are fine; frequent ones may affect your account).

### Step 2: Get Your Shipping Code

After you confirm, we will provide you with a **shipping code**. This appears:
- In your dashboard (on the order details page)
- In the confirmation email

The code is typically 6-8 characters. Save it or screenshot it — you will need it at the terminal.

### Step 3: Pack the Game

This is the most important step. A well-packed game arrives safely; a poorly packed game leads to disputes and bad reviews.

[See detailed packing instructions below](#how-to-pack-a-board-game)

### Step 4: Drop Off at Terminal

1. Go to any terminal in our network (Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, or uDrop)
2. On the terminal screen, select "Send a parcel" or "Enter code"
3. Enter your shipping code
4. A locker door will open
5. Place your package inside
6. Close the door
7. Done

The terminal will confirm the drop-off and tracking begins automatically.

### Step 5: Track and Complete

You can track the shipment in your dashboard. The buyer receives:
- Notification when you ship
- Notification when the parcel arrives at their terminal
- A PIN code to collect it

Once the buyer picks up the parcel:
- They have **2 days** to report any issues
- After 2 days (or when they confirm receipt), the order completes
- Your payment is released to your balance

## Shipping Timeline

| Step | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Confirm order | Within **24 hours** of purchase |
| Ship the game | Within **2 business days** of confirming |
| Delivery (within country) | **Next working day** |
| Delivery (cross-Baltic) | **2-3 working days** |
| Buyer pickup window | **72 hours** in locker |

**Business days** = Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.

### What Happens After 72 Hours?

If the buyer does not pick up within 72 hours:
1. Courier moves parcel to depot
2. Buyer receives SMS with redelivery link
3. Buyer has **4 days** to request redelivery to any terminal
4. If no action → parcel returns to sender

:::plain-terms
The game is shipped when you drop it at the terminal and the locker closes. That is when tracking begins and the clock stops. Do not wait until the last minute — terminal issues happen.
:::

## How to Pack a Board Game

Board games are sturdy, but they need proper packing to survive shipping. Here is how to do it right.

### The Golden Rules

1. **The box should not move** inside the shipping package
2. **Components should not move** inside the game box
3. **Corners need protection** — that is where damage happens
4. **Seal everything** — boxes pop open in transit

### Packing Materials You Will Need

| Material | Purpose | Where to get it |
|----------|---------|-----------------|
| **Outer box or mailer** | Protects the game box | Save boxes from online orders |
| **Bubble wrap or paper** | Padding and corner protection | Post office, shipping stores, free from shops |
| **Packing tape** | Seals everything shut | Any supermarket |
| **Rubber bands or bags** | Keeps components together inside | Optional but helpful |

### Step-by-Step Packing

**1. Secure the inside first**

Open the game and make sure components will not shift during transit:
- Bag loose tokens and pieces
- Rubber-band card decks
- If there is empty space in the box, add paper to fill it
- Put a rubber band around the game box (prevents lid from opening)

**2. Wrap the game box**

- Wrap in bubble wrap, focusing on corners
- Or wrap in kraft paper / newspaper as a base layer
- Make sure all corners have some protection

**3. Choose the right outer packaging**

| Game size | Outer packaging |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Small box (card games, small boxes) | Padded mailer or small box |
| Standard box (Catan-sized) | Shipping box with padding |
| Large/heavy box (Gloomhaven-sized) | Sturdy box with lots of padding |

**4. Fill empty space**

The game should fit snugly with no room to shift:
- Crumpled paper
- Bubble wrap
- Air pillows
- Foam sheets

**Shake test:** Pick up the package and shake it. If you hear or feel movement, add more padding.

**5. Seal it properly**

- Tape all seams of the outer box
- Use quality packing tape (not masking tape or regular tape)
- Consider taping the corners for extra strength

### Common Packing Mistakes

| Mistake | Better approach |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Using the game box as the shipping box | Always use an outer box — game boxes are not designed for shipping |
| Just putting the game in a plastic bag | No protection = damage |
| Oversized box with no padding | Game bounces around and gets damaged |
| Weak tape or no tape on game box lid | Lid opens, components scatter |
| Ignoring corner protection | Corners always take the hit — wrap them |

## Size and Weight Limits

Each parcel terminal network has its own locker sizes. Most board games fit in M or L compartments regardless of network.

### Unisend

| Size | Dimensions (H x W x D) | Fits |
|------|------------------------|------|
| **XS** | 8 x 18.5 x 61 cm | Envelopes, very small items |
| **S** | 8 x 35 x 61 cm | Card games, small flat boxes |
| **M** | 17.5 x 35 x 61 cm | Most standard board games |
| **L** | 36.5 x 35 x 61 cm | Large box games, multiple games |
| **XL** | 74.5 x 35 x 61 cm | Oversized games, big bundles |

**Weight limit:** 30 kg

### Latvijas Pasts

| Specification | Limit |
|---------------|-------|
| **Max dimensions** | 38 x 38 x 58 cm |
| **Max weight** | 30 kg |
| **Storage** | Up to **7 days** |
| **Insurance** | Up to €270 standard |

**Coverage:** Latvia only (but sends to Unisend lockers in Lithuania/Estonia via partnership)

### uDrop

| Specification | Details |
|---------------|---------|
| **Locker sizes** | XS, S, M, L, XL (similar to Unisend) |
| **Max weight** | 30 kg |
| **Storage** | 72 hours for recipient |
| **Coverage** | Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia, Poland |

### What Fits Where?

| Game type | Example | Works in all networks? |
|-----------|---------|------------------------|
| Card games | Exploding Kittens, Codenames | Yes |
| Small box | Azul, Patchwork, 7 Wonders | Yes |
| Standard box | Catan, Ticket to Ride | Yes |

## Troubleshooting

### The terminal rejected my package

**Possible reasons:**
- Package too large for available lockers → Try a different terminal with larger lockers
- Package too heavy → Check weight limits
- Code already used → Contact support
- Terminal malfunction → Try another terminal nearby

### My code does not work

- Double-check you are entering it correctly (watch for O vs 0, I vs 1)
- Make sure you are at a terminal in our network (Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, or uDrop)
- Check your dashboard — has the code expired or been cancelled?
- Contact support if the issue persists

### The buyer says they did not receive it

1. Check tracking in your dashboard
2. If tracking shows "delivered to terminal" — the buyer needs to check their notifications for the pickup code
3. If tracking shows stuck/delayed — contact support
4. Keep all tracking information for your records

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to print a label?

**No.** Our system uses codes, not labels. Just enter your code at the terminal — no printer needed.

### Can I ship internationally?

Currently, we only support shipping within the Baltic region (Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia) via parcel terminals. For buyers outside this area, use Contact Seller listings and arrange shipping directly.

### What if I miss the 2-day shipping deadline?

The buyer may cancel for a full refund, and your account may be flagged. If you know you will be late, communicate with the buyer — many will understand if you are upfront about it.

### Can I use my own shipping method?

For Instant Buy orders: **No.** You must use our parcel terminal network — that is how we provide tracking and buyer protection.

For Contact Seller orders: You arrange shipping directly with the buyer however you both agree.

### What if the game is damaged in transit?

If a buyer reports damage:
1. Review the photos they provide
2. If damage is from poor packing, you may be liable
3. If damage is clearly from carrier mishandling, contact support

**Claims window:** You can submit a claim for lost or damaged parcels within **6 months** from the date sent.

## Shipping Checklist

Before every shipment:

**Packing:**
- Components secured inside game box
- Game box wrapped or protected
- Outer packaging appropriate size
- All empty space filled with padding
- Corners protected
- All seams taped

**Shipping:**
- Order confirmed within 24 hours
- Shipping code saved
- Dropped at terminal within 2 business days
- Locker closed and confirmed
