Welcome to the Second Turn Games Help Centre. Here you will find step-by-step guides for everything you need to know about using our marketplace.

## Quick Answers

**Who can use Second Turn Games?**
Anyone aged 16+ can browse and buy games. You must be 18+ to sell using our Claim this game feature. We currently serve the Baltic region (Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia).

**How do listings work?**
Sellers list games with photos, a condition grade, and a price. Buyers can either claim games instantly (with secure payment and shipping through the platform) or message the seller to arrange things directly.

**What does it cost?**
Buyers pay the listed price plus a flat €2.00 shipping fee. Sellers pay a 10% commission on completed sales. There are no listing fees and no hidden costs. See our [Payments & Fees](/legal/fees) page for full details.

**Is my payment protected?**
For Claim this game orders, yes. We hold the payment until you receive and check the game. You have 2 days after delivery to report any problems. See our [buying guide](/help/buying) for details.

**Are sellers businesses?**
No. All sellers on Second Turn Games are private individuals selling from their personal collections. This means EU consumer protection laws (14-day returns, 2-year guarantee) do not apply. Our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) explain what protections are in place instead.

**What about taxes?**
Most casual sellers are not affected. EU rules (DAC7) only require us to report sellers who exceed 30 sales or €2,000 in a year. See our [DAC7 guide](/help/dac7) for the full explanation.
