## The Short Version

**Clearing out your game shelf? You are probably fine.**

Most sellers on Second Turn Games will never be reported to tax authorities. The EU reporting rules (called DAC7) only kick in for high-volume sellers — and even then, being reported does not mean you owe extra taxes.

:::plain-terms
In the past 12 months, have you sold 30+ items on our platform? Or earned €2,000+ total? If BOTH answers are "no", you will not be reported. Most casual sellers are well below these thresholds.
:::

---

## What is DAC7?

DAC7 is an EU directive (officially: Council Directive 2021/514) that requires online marketplaces to share seller information with tax authorities. It applies to **all** EU platforms — eBay, Vinted, Etsy, Airbnb — not just us.

The goal is tax transparency: helping tax authorities see income earned through online platforms. It has been in effect since January 2023.

**Important:** DAC7 is about **information sharing**, not new taxes. Being reported does not automatically mean you owe anything.

## The Thresholds Explained

We only report your information if you exceed **at least one** of these thresholds in a calendar year:

| Threshold | Amount | What it means |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| **Sales count** | 30 or more | You have sold 30+ items |
| **Total proceeds** | €2,000 or more | Your total sales add up to €2,000+ |

### You only need to cross one threshold

You only need to cross **one** threshold to be reported:

| Your activity | Reported? | Why |
|---------------|-----------|-----|
| 12 sales, €350 total | No | Below both thresholds |
| 25 sales, €1,800 total | No | Below both thresholds |
| 28 sales, €2,100 total | Yes | Over €2,000 |
| 35 sales, €900 total | Yes | Over 30 sales |
| 40 sales, €3,000 total | Yes | Over both thresholds |

### What counts as a "sale"?

- Completed Instant Buy transactions
- Cancelled or refunded orders do not count
- Each order is one sale (regardless of how many games are in it)

### What counts toward "proceeds"?

- The listing price you receive (before any Stripe fees)
- Shipping fees paid by buyers do not count toward your proceeds

## What Happens If I Am Reported?

If you exceed either threshold, we report the following to Latvia's State Revenue Service (VID) by January 31 of the following year:

| Data | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Your full legal name | As registered in your account |
| Your address | Primary residential address |
| Your date of birth | For identification |
| Your Tax ID (TIN) | Your personas kods / personal code |
| Your bank account (IBAN) | The account linked to your payouts |
| Your quarterly sales totals | Amount and number of transactions |

VID then shares this with tax authorities in your country of residence (if different from Latvia) through EU automatic exchange systems.

### What happens next?

Honestly? **For most people, nothing.**

Tax authorities receive this data alongside information from many other platforms. They may:
- Do nothing (most common for small amounts)
- Cross-reference with your tax returns
- Ask questions if something does not match

If you have been declaring your income correctly, you have nothing to worry about.

## "But Do I Owe Taxes on My Sales?"

**We cannot give tax advice** — but here is general context:

### Selling personal items

If you are selling board games from your personal collection (things you bought to play, not to resell), this is generally **not taxable income** in most EU countries. You are just recovering some of what you originally paid.

Think of it this way: if you bought a game for €40, played it for two years, and sold it for €25, you have not made money — you have taken a loss.

### When it might be different

Tax obligations may apply if you are:
- Buying games specifically to resell them for profit
- Selling at a scale that looks like a business
- Already registered as a business

### The honest taxpayer message

Latvia's State Revenue Service (VID) has said it clearly:

> *"For sellers who honestly pay taxes, the new directive and regulations will change nothing, and they will not feel it in their daily lives."*

If you are already compliant, DAC7 is just paperwork for us, not a problem for you.

## We Will Warn You Before You Cross

You will never be surprised. Here is how our warning system works:

| Your progress | What we do |
|---------------|------------|
| 0-79% of either threshold | Nothing — sell freely |
| ~80% (24 sales or €1,600) | We notify you and request your TIN |
| 100% (30 sales or €2,000) | We report to VID in January |

### What is a TIN?

Your Tax Identification Number. In the Baltics:

| Country | TIN format |
|---------|------------|
| **Latvia** | Personas kods (11 digits, on your ID card) |
| **Lithuania** | Asmens kodas (11 digits) |
| **Estonia** | Isikukood (11 digits) |

If you do not have a TIN (rare for residents), we can use your place of birth instead.

### What if I do not provide my TIN?

If you exceed a threshold without providing your TIN:
- Your selling privileges will be paused
- Pending payouts will be frozen
- We will still report what we have (without TIN)

Just provide it when asked — it takes 30 seconds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does being reported mean I owe taxes?

**No.** Reporting is just information sharing. Whether you owe taxes depends on your personal situation, not on whether we reported you.

### Can I avoid being reported by using multiple accounts?

No — and do not try. Creating multiple accounts to evade reporting thresholds violates our Terms of Service and may be illegal. We detect and merge duplicate accounts.

### What if I sell on other platforms too?

Each platform reports independently. If you sell 20 items on Second Turn Games and 20 on Vinted, both platforms stay below 30 — neither reports you. But tax authorities may combine this data on their end.

### I am not a tax resident of Latvia. Does this still apply?

Yes. If you are an EU resident selling on our platform, you will be reported to VID, who then shares with your country's tax authority.

### Can I see what you reported about me?

Yes. If you exceed thresholds, we will send you a copy of the report we submitted. You can also request this from your account settings.

### I made a mistake in my account information. What do I do?

Update your account information immediately. Accurate data protects you. If we have already submitted a report with incorrect information, contact us at info@secondturn.games.
