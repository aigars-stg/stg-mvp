## The Short Version

1. **Find your game** → Search by name or browse by category, player count, price
2. **Check the listing** → Read condition grade, photos, and seller description
3. **Add to cart** → For Instant Buy listings, click "Add to Cart"
4. **Choose your parcel terminal** → Pick a convenient pickup point
5. **Pay** → Card, bank link, wallet balance, or a combination
6. **Pick up your game** → Collect from your parcel terminal with the PIN code you receive

**What you pay:** Item price + €2.00 flat shipping. No service fees, no hidden costs.

## Finding Games

### Search

Start typing a game name in the search bar. Our autocomplete pulls from the BoardGameGeek database of 170,000+ games, so even niche titles will show up.

### Browse and Filter

Narrow things down with filters:
- **Category** — Strategy, family, party, cooperative, and more
- **Player count** — Find games for your group size
- **Price range** — Set a minimum and maximum
- **Condition** — Like New, Very Good, Good, Acceptable
- **Language** — Filter by the edition's language
- **Location** — Seller's country (Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia)

### What You See on a Listing

Each listing shows:
- **Game details** pulled from BoardGameGeek (player count, playing time, age rating, description)
- **Seller's photos** of the actual copy
- **Condition grade** with notes on any wear or missing components
- **Price** in EUR
- **Seller profile** with their rating and number of sales
- **Listing type** — Instant Buy or Contact Seller

## Understanding Listings

### Condition Grades

Every listing uses one of four grades. Sellers grade their own games, but accurate grading is enforced through the dispute system.

| Grade | What It Means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Like New** | Appears unplayed or played once. Complete, no visible wear. |
| **Very Good** | Played with care, minimal wear. Complete, fully functional. |
| **Good** | Regular play, normal wear. Complete, fully functional. |
| **Acceptable** | Significant wear or missing components. Issues clearly described. |

If a listing is graded Acceptable, the seller must describe exactly what is wrong — missing pieces, box damage, or other issues. Photos are required for Acceptable listings.

See also: [Condition Grading Guide](/help?section=grading) for detailed descriptions with examples

### Instant Buy vs Contact Seller

There are two types of listings, and they work quite differently.

**Instant Buy:**
- Fixed price — click, pay, done
- Payment handled through the platform (card, bank link, or wallet)
- Shipped via parcel terminal (Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, uDrop)
- Flat €2.00 shipping fee
- Full buyer protection with 2-day dispute window

**Contact Seller:**
- You message the seller first to discuss
- Arrange payment and delivery directly between yourselves
- More flexible (negotiate price, arrange local pickup, etc.)
- No platform payment protection

:::plain-terms
Instant Buy means click, pay, done. Contact Seller means you message first and work things out directly — price, payment, and delivery are between you and the seller.
:::

## How to Purchase (Instant Buy)

### Step 1: Add to Cart

Click **"Add to Cart"** on any Instant Buy listing. You can add games from multiple sellers — each seller's items will become a separate order at checkout.

### Step 2: Review Your Cart

Your cart shows:
- Each item with its price and condition
- Shipping fee (€2.00 per seller order)
- Total amount

Remove items or continue shopping before proceeding to checkout.

### Step 3: Choose Your Parcel Terminal

Select a pickup terminal from the available networks:
- **Unisend** — Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia
- **Latvijas Pasts** — Latvia
- **uDrop** — Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia

Pick whichever terminal is most convenient for you — near home, near work, wherever suits you best.

### Step 4: Pay

Choose how to pay and complete the transaction.

### Payment Methods

| Method | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Cards** | Visa and Mastercard via EveryPay |
| **Bank links** | Baltic banks (Swedbank, SEB, Citadele, Luminor, and others) via EveryPay |
| **Wallet balance** | From previous refunds or from selling your own games |

### Using Wallet Balance

If you have wallet balance (from refunds or from selling games on the platform), it is applied first automatically:
- **Wallet covers the full amount** → No card or bank payment needed. The order is created instantly.
- **Wallet covers part of the amount** → The remaining balance is charged to your card or bank link.
- **No wallet balance** → Full amount charged to your chosen payment method.

### Step 5: Order Created

Once payment is confirmed, your order is created and the seller is notified. You will receive a confirmation email with your order details.

## After You Buy

Here is what happens after you complete your purchase.

### 1. Seller Confirms (Within 24 Hours)

The seller has 24 hours to confirm they can fulfill the order. Most sellers confirm within a few hours.

### 2. Seller Ships (Within 2 Business Days)

After confirming, the seller packs your game and drops it at a parcel terminal. You will receive a notification when the parcel is on its way.

### 3. Parcel Arrives at Your Terminal

Depending on the route:
- **Same country:** Usually next working day
- **Cross-Baltic:** 2-3 working days

You will receive a notification with a **PIN code** to collect your parcel.

### 4. Pick Up Your Game

Go to your chosen parcel terminal, enter your PIN code, and collect your game. The locker door opens and the game is yours.

**Pickup window:** You have **72 hours** to collect. After that, the parcel may be moved to a depot with a redelivery option.

### 5. Check the Game (2-Day Window)

Once you have picked up the game, you have **2 days** to check it against the listing description. Open the box, verify the components, inspect the condition.

If everything looks good — no action needed. The order auto-completes after 2 days.

### 6. Order Completes

After the 2-day window, the order is marked complete and the seller receives their payment. At this point, the transaction is final.

:::plain-terms
You have 2 days after delivery to check the game. If something is wrong — not as described, damaged, missing pieces — report it before the 2 days are up. After that, the order is marked complete and the seller gets paid.
:::

## If Something Goes Wrong

### Opening a Dispute

If the game is not as described, you can open a dispute within the 2-day window after delivery.

**How to report a problem:**
1. Go to your **Orders** page
2. Find the order and click **"Report a Problem"**
3. Describe the issue clearly
4. Upload photos showing the problem
5. Submit — our team will review it

### Valid Reasons for Disputes

| Reason | Example |
|--------|---------|
| **Not as described** | Listed as "Very Good" but has significant damage |
| **Missing components** | Components missing that were not disclosed in the listing |
| **Damaged in transit** | Box crushed, components broken during shipping |
| **Wrong item** | Received a different game or edition than listed |

### What Is Not a Valid Dispute

- Changed your mind about the purchase
- You found it cheaper elsewhere
- You do not like the game
- Minor wear consistent with the listed condition grade

### What Happens Next

1. Our team reviews the evidence — your report, the listing photos, and the seller's response
2. **If valid:** You receive a full refund (card payments go back to your card, wallet payments go back to your wallet)
3. **If unclear:** We may ask both sides for more information before deciding
4. **If not valid:** The order completes as normal

### After the 2-Day Window

Once the 2-day window has passed:
- The order is marked complete and the seller has been paid
- Platform disputes are no longer available
- You can still contact the seller directly through messages
- If that does not resolve things, contact your bank about a chargeback as a last resort

## How Contact Seller Listings Work

For Contact Seller listings, the process is different — there is no cart, no checkout, and no platform payment.

### Step 1: Message the Seller

Click **"Contact Seller"** on the listing page. Write a message to the seller — introduce yourself, ask questions about the game, or make an offer.

### Step 2: Discuss and Agree

Chat with the seller to:
- Ask about condition, completeness, or anything not covered in the listing
- Negotiate price if you wish
- Agree on payment method (bank transfer, cash on pickup, etc.)
- Arrange delivery (local pickup, postal service, etc.)

### Step 3: Complete the Transaction

Payment and delivery are between you and the seller. Common approaches:
- **Local pickup:** Meet in person, inspect the game, pay cash
- **Bank transfer:** Buyer sends payment, seller ships the game
- **Other payment apps:** Whatever you both agree on

**Important:** Contact Seller transactions do not have platform buyer protection. Choose payment methods you are comfortable with, and consider meeting in a public place for local pickups.

## Tips for Buyers

### Before You Buy

- **Read the condition description carefully** — the grade and notes tell you what to expect
- **Look at all photos** — zoom in on corners, edges, and any areas of wear
- **Check if components are listed as complete** — and read the description for any noted omissions
- **Ask the seller questions** — use the message button if anything is unclear
- **Look at seller reviews** — feedback from previous buyers is a good indicator

### Choosing a Parcel Terminal

- Pick a terminal convenient for your daily routine (near home, work, or your commute)
- Available networks: Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, uDrop — over 1,000 terminals across the Baltics
- You will receive a PIN code when the parcel arrives, with a 72-hour pickup window
- You can save a preferred terminal in your account settings

### Getting the Best Value

- **Check the BGG data** — compare the listing price to the original retail price
- **Consider bundled expansions** — listings with included expansions can be great value
- **Condition matters** — a "Good" copy at the right price can be a better deal than "Like New" at near-retail

## Common Questions

### Can I return a game?

Sellers are private individuals, so EU consumer return rights (14-day withdrawal) do not apply to peer-to-peer transactions. You can open a dispute within 2 days of delivery if the item is significantly not as described, but "changed my mind" is not a valid reason for a return.

### Can I buy from another country?

Yes. You can buy from sellers in Latvia, Lithuania, and Estonia. Shipping is the same flat €2.00 across the Baltics, regardless of whether the seller is in your country or not.

### What if the seller does not ship?

If a seller does not confirm within 24 hours or does not ship within 2 business days after confirming, the order is cancelled and you receive a full automatic refund.

### Can I message a seller before buying?

Yes — use the message button on any listing to ask questions about condition, completeness, or anything else before committing to a purchase.

### How do refunds work?

| Original payment | Refund method | Timing |
|------------------|---------------|--------|
| Card (Visa/Mastercard) | Back to your card | 3-5 business days |
| Bank link | Back to your bank account | 3-5 business days |
| Wallet balance | Back to your wallet | Instant |

If you paid with a combination of wallet and card, each portion is refunded to its original source.

### Do I need an account to buy?

Yes. You need a verified email address and must be at least 16 years old.

### Can I combine items from different sellers?

Yes. You can add Instant Buy items from multiple sellers to your cart. At checkout, each seller's items become a separate order with its own shipping fee and tracking.

### What if the parcel terminal is full?

If your chosen terminal has no available lockers when the carrier arrives, the parcel will be redirected to the nearest terminal with space. You will be notified of the updated pickup location.
