## The Short Version

1. **Find your game** → Search by name, we pull details from BoardGameGeek
2. **Describe condition** → Four grades, note any missing pieces
3. **Add photos** → Optional for most conditions, required for Acceptable
4. **Set your price** → Use our pricing assistant for market data
5. **Choose how to sell** → Instant Buy (we handle payment) or Contact Seller (you arrange)
6. **Publish** → Your listing goes live immediately

**What you will earn:** 90% of your listing price. We deduct a 10% platform commission. Buyers pay no service fee.

## Step 1: Find Your Game

Go to **[Sell](/sell)** and search for your game by name.

We use BoardGameGeek's database of 170,000+ games. When you select a game, we automatically fill in:
- Game name and year
- Player count and playing time
- Images and description
- Available versions/editions

### Selecting the Right Version

Games often have multiple editions and language versions. Pick the one that matches your copy — this helps buyers know exactly what they are getting.

If your specific version is not listed, you can add details manually.

### Adding Expansions

Selling the base game with expansions included? Add them in the **Included Expansions** section. This is a great way to bundle and increase value.

:::plain-terms
You can go directly to `/sell?q=catan` to start with a search already filled in. Handy if you are listing multiple games.
:::

## Step 2: Grade the Condition

Be honest — accurate grading builds trust and prevents disputes.

### The Four Grades

| Grade | What It Means | Components | Wear |
|-------|---------------|------------|------|
| **Like New** | Pristine, minimal play | Complete | None visible |
| **Very Good** | Light use, well cared for | Complete | Minor |
| **Good** | Regular play, fully functional | Complete | Normal |
| **Acceptable** | Incomplete or significant wear | May be missing pieces | Noticeable |

### Component Checklist

You will confirm whether **all components are present**. If anything is missing:
1. Mark "No" on the components question
2. Describe what is missing (up to 500 characters)
3. The listing automatically becomes **Acceptable** grade

### Condition Notes

For **Acceptable** condition, you must add notes explaining the issues. Be specific:

| Vague | Specific |
|---------|-----------|
| "Some wear" | "Box corners worn, one card has small crease" |
| "Missing pieces" | "Missing 2 red meeples, replaced with wooden cubes" |
| "Some wear and tear" | "Rulebook has coffee stain on cover, rules readable" |

**Golden rule:** When in doubt, grade lower. Buyers appreciate honesty, and you will avoid disputes.

See also: [Condition Grading Guide](/help?section=grading) for detailed examples

## Step 3: Add Photos

Photos help your listing stand out and give buyers confidence.

### Requirements

| Condition | Photos Required? |
|-----------|------------------|
| Like New | Optional (but recommended) |
| Very Good | Optional (but recommended) |
| Good | Optional (but recommended) |
| **Acceptable** | **Required (minimum 1)** |

### Photo Limits

- **Maximum:** 8 photos per listing
- **File size:** Up to 5MB each
- **Formats:** JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF

### What to Photograph

1. **Box front** — Shows overall condition
2. **Box back** — Often has wear
3. **Components** — Laid out clearly
4. **Any damage** — Be upfront about issues
5. **Inside the box** — Shows organization/storage
6. **Close-ups** — Of any wear or damage

### Privacy Note

We automatically strip all metadata (GPS location, camera info, etc.) from your photos before storing them.

## Step 4: Set Your Price

### Fixed Price vs Auction

| | Fixed Price | Auction |
|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | Quick sales, known value | Rare games, price discovery |
| **Buyer experience** | Buy immediately | Bid and wait |
| **Price control** | You set the exact price | Market determines final price |

### Fixed Price

Enter your asking price in EUR. Minimum: any amount above €0.

### Auction Settings

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| Starting bid | Minimum €1.00 |
| Duration | 1, 3, 5, or 7 days |
| Bid increment | €1.00 minimum |

**No reserve price** — the starting bid is the minimum the game will sell for.

**Anti-snipe protection** — If someone bids in the final 5 minutes, the auction extends by 2-5 minutes to give others a chance.

### Using the Pricing Assistant

Click **"Get pricing help"** to see:
- Retail prices (from BoardGamePrices.co.uk)
- Lowest active listing on our platform
- Median and average sold prices
- Number of recent sales

The assistant can **auto-fill a suggested price** based on your condition grade.

:::plain-terms
Check what similar copies have sold for, not just listed for. Factor in condition — Very Good is not Like New; price accordingly. Bundled expansions add value. Being slightly under market often sells faster.
:::

## Step 5: Choose How to Sell

### Option A: Contact Seller

Buyers message you to discuss the purchase. You arrange payment and shipping directly.

**Pros:**
- No setup required
- Flexible payment options
- You control the process

**Cons:**
- More back-and-forth
- No buyer protection
- You handle payment collection

**Best for:** High-value items, local pickup, international buyers, sellers who prefer direct communication.

### Option B: Instant Buy

Buyers purchase immediately. We handle payment, you ship via parcel terminal.

**Pros:**
- One-click purchase for buyers
- Guaranteed payment
- Automatic shipping labels
- Buyer protection builds trust

**Cons:**
- Requires seller verification
- Must ship within 2 business days
- Limited to Baltic parcel terminals

**Best for:** Standard games, hands-off selling, building trust as a new seller.

### Setting Up Instant Buy

If you have not completed seller verification yet, you will see an "Upgrade to Instant Buy" prompt. The setup takes a few minutes:

1. Accept the Seller Agreement
2. Complete identity verification (name, date of birth, address)
3. You are ready to list Instant Buy items

Your bank details (IBAN) will be collected when you request your first withdrawal — not during setup.

## When Someone Buys (Instant Buy)

### 1. You Get Notified

You will receive:
- **Email:** "New Order — Action Required"
- **Dashboard alert:** Order appears in your Seller Orders

### 2. Confirm the Order (24 Hours)

You have **24 hours** to confirm you can fulfill the order. This is your chance to verify you still have the game.

**If you do not respond in 24 hours:** The order is automatically cancelled and the buyer is refunded.

### 3. Ship the Game (2 Business Days)

After confirming:
1. We generate your shipping label automatically
2. You receive an email with the parcel code
3. Pack the game securely
4. Take it to any Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, or uDrop terminal
5. Enter your code and place the parcel in the locker

See also: [Shipping Guide](/help?section=shipping) for packing tips and terminal details

### 4. Tracking Updates Automatically

We sync with the carrier — no need to manually mark as shipped.

### 5. Get Paid

**2 days after delivery**, the order completes and your earnings (listing price minus 10% commission) are credited to your platform wallet.

Withdraw to your bank account at any time from your dashboard — minimum €5, no fees, processed within 1-3 business days.

See also: [Fees and Payouts](/legal?section=fees) for payout details

## Auctions

Auctions let the market decide the price. They work well for rare, out-of-print, or hard-to-price games where you suspect demand may exceed your estimate.

### Setting Up an Auction

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Starting bid** | Minimum €1.00 — this is the lowest the game can sell for |
| **Duration** | 1, 3, 5, or 7 days |
| **Bid increment** | €1.00 minimum — each new bid must be at least this much higher |

There is no reserve price. The starting bid is effectively your minimum — set it at the lowest amount you would accept.

:::plain-terms
If you start an auction at €5 and only one person bids €5, that is the sale price. Set your starting bid at a number you are comfortable selling for.
:::

### How Bidding Works

- Buyers place bids on the listing page
- Each bid must be at least €1.00 higher than the current highest bid
- The current bid amount and number of bids are visible to everyone
- Bidders are notified when they are outbid

### Anti-Snipe Protection

If someone places a bid in the final **5 minutes** of an auction, the end time extends by **2-5 minutes**. This prevents last-second bidding ("sniping") and gives other bidders a fair chance to respond.

The auction only ends when no new bids have been placed in the final 5 minutes.

### During the Auction

- Current bid and bid count visible to all
- You can answer questions via messages
- Real-time updates as bids come in
- You **cannot** cancel an auction that has bids

### When the Auction Ends

**With bids:**
1. The highest bidder wins and is notified immediately
2. They have **48 hours** to complete payment
3. If they do not pay within 48 hours, the second-highest bidder gets a chance (also 48 hours)
4. Once paid, the order proceeds like any Instant Buy — you confirm, pack, and ship

**Without bids:**
- The auction ends with no winner
- You can relist as a fixed-price listing or start a new auction
- No fees are charged for auctions that receive no bids

### Auction Tips

- **Start low if you are confident in demand** — a low starting bid attracts more watchers and bids
- **Start at your minimum if unsure** — remember, one bid at your starting price means a sale at that price
- **7-day auctions** get the most exposure, but 3-day works well for popular games
- **Good photos matter even more** — bidders need confidence to bid higher
- **Mention the edition and language** — bidders want to know exactly what they are bidding on

## In Search Of (ISO) Feature

Buyers can post "wanted" listings for games they are looking for. As a seller, you can browse these and respond if you have what they want.

### How It Works

1. Go to **[Wanted](/wanted)** to browse ISO listings
2. Find a game you have
3. Click "I Have This"
4. Enter your price and condition
5. A conversation starts with the buyer

**Limit:** You can respond to up to 10 wanted listings, and only once per listing.

This is a great way to find buyers for games you are about to list — they are actively looking.

## Common Questions

### Can I sell internationally?

**Contact Seller:** Yes — arrange shipping directly with the buyer.
**Instant Buy:** Currently Baltic region only (Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia) via parcel terminals.

### What if I cannot fulfill an order?

**Before confirming:** Decline the order. The buyer gets a full refund.
**After confirming:** Contact support immediately. We will help resolve it.

### How do disputes work?

Buyers can open a dispute within 2 days of delivery if the item is not as described. Our support team reviews and resolves disputes. Keep your photos and descriptions accurate to avoid this.

### Is there a listing limit?

You can create up to **50 listings per day**. There is no limit on active listings.

### Do listings expire?

No. Listings stay active until sold or you remove them.

### What fees do I pay?

A 10% platform commission is deducted from your item price for each Instant Buy sale. For a game listed at €25, you receive €22.50. Buyers pay the listed price plus €2 shipping — they pay no service fee.

Contact Seller listings have no platform fees.

See also: [Fees](/legal?section=fees)
