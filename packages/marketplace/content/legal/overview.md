## Quick Answers

### Who can use Second Turn Games?

Anyone 16 or older in Latvia, Lithuania, or Estonia can browse and buy. To sell, you must be at least 18.

:::plain-terms
The 18+ requirement for selling comes from payment regulations. We need to verify your identity to comply with financial and tax reporting requirements.
:::

---

### How do listings work?

Sellers can choose how they want to sell:

**Instant Buy** — Set a fixed price. Buyers purchase immediately and you ship the game. Simple and quick.

**Contact Seller** — Buyers message you first to discuss price or ask questions. You arrange payment and delivery directly. Good for negotiation or when you are flexible on price.

Both listing types are free to create. You can have multiple active listings at once.

---

### What does it cost?

**Buyers pay the listed price plus €2.00 flat shipping.** No service fees, no hidden costs.

**Sellers pay a 10% commission** on the item price, deducted from earnings.

**Example — a €20 game:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Game price | €20.00 |
| Shipping | €2.00 |
| **Buyer pays** | **€22.00** |
| Commission (10%) | -€2.00 |
| **Seller receives** | **€18.00** |

**Withdrawals to your bank:** Your earnings go to your platform wallet. Withdraw to your bank account at any time — no fees.

:::plain-terms
Buyers pay the listed price plus €2 shipping — that is it, no surprise fees at checkout. Sellers pay 10% commission, and can withdraw earnings to their bank for free.
:::

### How are payments protected?

Payment is processed when you buy, and the seller receives funds after the order completes. Here is how it works:

1. **You pay** — Payment is processed securely via card or bank link
2. **Seller ships** — Within 2 business days
3. **You receive** — Check the game matches the description
4. **Order completes** — After 2 days, or when you confirm earlier
5. **Seller gets paid** — Funds are transferred to their balance

If something is wrong, you have **2 days after delivery** to open a dispute.

:::plain-terms
The seller does not get your money immediately. You have 2 days after delivery to check the game and report any problems.
:::

### Is this like buying from a shop?

No. All sellers on Second Turn Games are private individuals, not businesses.

**This means:**
- EU consumer protection laws (like the 14-day return right) do not apply
- There is no 2-year legal guarantee
- You are buying from another person, not a company

We provide the platform and payment protection, but we are not a party to your transaction. Think of us as the trusted venue where you meet, not the seller.

:::plain-terms
This is more like buying at a flea market than from a shop. Check the photos carefully, ask questions, and read the condition description before you buy.
:::

---

### Do I need to worry about tax reporting?

**Most sellers will not be affected.** If you are just clearing out your game shelf occasionally, you will likely stay well below the reporting thresholds.

EU rules (DAC7) require us to report seller information to tax authorities only if you exceed **either** threshold in a calendar year:
- **30 or more sales**, OR
- **€2,000 or more** in total proceeds

If you stay below **both** thresholds, we do not report anything about you.

:::plain-terms
Sold 12 games for €350? Not reported. Sold 25 games for €1,800? Not reported. But 35 games for €900? Reported (over 30 sales). And if you already pay taxes on your income, this changes nothing for you — it is just information sharing.
:::

We will warn you when you are approaching either threshold (~80%) so you are never surprised.

### What data do you collect?

The short version:
- **Account info**: Name, email, country
- **Sellers**: Identity details (required by law for tax reporting)
- **Transactions**: Order history, messages, reviews
- **Technical**: IP address, device info (for security)

We never sell your data. We share it only with services that help us run the platform (payments, shipping, email) and when required by law.

## Legal Framework

Second Turn Games operates under:

- **GDPR** (EU 2016/679) — Data protection
- **DAC7** (EU 2021/514) — Platform seller reporting
- **DSA** (Digital Services Act) — Platform transparency
- **Latvian law** — Governing law for all agreements

Disputes are resolved in the courts of Riga, Latvia.
