## 1. Our Fee Philosophy

Second Turn Games keeps pricing simple and transparent. Buyers pay the listed price plus flat-rate shipping — no service fees, no surprises at checkout. Sellers pay a 10% commission on the item price.

:::plain-terms
What you see is what you pay. If a game is listed for €25 and shipping is €2, you pay €27. That is it. Sellers receive €22.50 (the listing price minus 10% commission).
:::

## 2. Fee Summary

### For Buyers

| Cost | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Game price | As listed by seller |
| Shipping | **€2.00** (flat rate) |
| Service fee | **None** |

You pay the listed price plus €2.00 shipping. No hidden fees.

You can also use your **wallet balance** to pay for purchases. If your wallet covers the full amount, no card payment is needed.

### For Sellers

| Fee | Amount |
|-----|--------|
| Listing fee | **Free** |
| Platform commission | **10% of item price** |
| Withdrawal to bank | **Free** |

Your earnings (item price minus 10% commission) are credited to your platform wallet after each completed sale.

### For Contact Seller Transactions

No platform fees apply. Any costs depend on how you and the seller arrange payment and delivery.

## 4. Fee Breakdown

### 4.1 Platform Commission (10%)

The 10% commission covers:
- **Secure payment processing** — Card and bank link payments processed safely
- **Buyer protection** — Payment is held until delivery is confirmed
- **Platform operation** — Hosting, development, customer support
- **Fraud prevention** — We monitor for scams and bad actors
- **Shipping integration** — Automated label generation and tracking

The commission is calculated on the item price only (not on shipping).

:::plain-terms
We charge sellers 10% so we can keep the buyer experience simple — no surprise fees at checkout. The commission covers all the behind-the-scenes work: payment processing, shipping labels, platform development, and keeping everyone safe.
:::

### 4.2 Shipping (€2.00)

The flat €2.00 shipping fee covers delivery via parcel terminal anywhere in Latvia, Lithuania, or Estonia.

**What is included:**
- Terminal-to-terminal delivery (Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, or uDrop)
- Tracking
- Insurance for standard-value items

**Same price everywhere** — Whether you are shipping across town or across the Baltics, it is €2.00.

:::plain-terms
€2 flat shipping across the Baltics is one of the best deals around. No weight calculations, no distance surcharges. Just €2, whether it is Riga to Vilnius or Tallinn to Liepaja.
:::

## 5. Wallet and Withdrawals

### 5.1 Your Platform Wallet

After each completed sale, your earnings are credited to your platform wallet. You can:

- **Buy games** — Use your wallet balance for purchases on the marketplace
- **Withdraw to your bank** — Request a transfer to your bank account at any time

### 5.2 Withdrawals

| Detail | |
|--------|---|
| Minimum withdrawal | €5.00 |
| Withdrawal fee | **Free** |
| Processing time | 1-3 business days |
| Method | IBAN bank transfer |

Your IBAN is collected when you request your first withdrawal — not during registration.

### 5.3 When You Get Paid

After an Instant Buy order:

1. Buyer purchases → Payment processed
2. You confirm and ship
3. Buyer receives the game
4. Order completes (2 days after delivery, or when buyer confirms earlier)
5. **Earnings credited to your wallet** (item price minus 10% commission)

:::plain-terms
Your money goes to your wallet after each completed sale. From there, you can spend it on games or withdraw it to your bank account whenever you like. No minimum balance, no fees, no waiting for a threshold.
:::

### 5.4 Small Balances

If your balance is below €5 and you want to close your account, contact us — we will transfer any remaining balance regardless of the minimum.

## 6. Auctions

Auction fees work the same as fixed-price listings:

- **Instant Buy auction** — Buyer pays winning bid + €2.00 shipping. Seller pays 10% commission on the winning bid.
- **Contact Seller auction** — No platform fees; arrange payment directly with seller.
