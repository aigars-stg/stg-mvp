## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Second Turn Games. This guide explains how buying works on our platform, what protections you have, and what to expect when purchasing pre-loved board games from other members of our community.

**Important:** All sellers on Second Turn Games are private individuals, not businesses. This affects your rights as a buyer — please read Section 2 carefully.

---

## 2. Buying from Private Sellers

### 2.1 What This Means for You

Second Turn Games is a peer-to-peer marketplace. Every seller is a private individual selling games from their personal collection — not a shop or business.

:::plain-terms
Buying here is more like buying at a flea market than from a shop. You are buying directly from another person. That means some consumer protections you would get from a business do not apply here.
:::

### 2.2 What You Do Not Have

When buying from a private seller:

- **No 14-day return right** — You cannot return an item just because you changed your mind
- **No 2-year legal guarantee** — The EU conformity guarantee does not apply
- **No automatic refunds** — Returns depend on the seller's willingness or a valid dispute

### 2.3 What You Do Have

- **Honest listings** — Sellers must accurately describe condition and disclose defects
- **Actual photos** — Listings show the real item, not stock images
- **Payment protection** — For Instant Buy orders, the seller receives funds only after delivery is confirmed
- **2-day dispute window** — Report problems within 2 days of delivery
- **Platform support** — We investigate fraud and ban bad actors

*This information is provided in accordance with EU Directive 2019/2161 (Omnibus Directive).*

## 3. Two Ways to Buy

### 3.1 Instant Buy

Instant Buy listings let you purchase immediately with full platform protection.

**How it works:**
1. You find a game and click "Buy Now"
2. You pay securely via card or bank link
3. The seller confirms and ships within 2 business days
4. You collect from your chosen parcel terminal (Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, or uDrop)
5. Check the game matches the description
6. Order completes automatically 2 days after delivery

**What you get:**
- Secure payment processing
- Shipping to any parcel terminal in Latvia, Lithuania, or Estonia
- 2-day window to report issues before the seller receives payment
- Tracking for your order

:::plain-terms
Instant Buy is the safest way to buy. Your payment is processed and the seller only receives funds after you have had a chance to check the game. If something is wrong, you have 2 days to report it.
:::

### 3.2 Contact Seller

Contact Seller listings require you to message the seller first.

**How it works:**
1. You find a game and click "Contact Seller"
2. You message the seller to discuss price, condition, or ask questions
3. If you agree, you arrange payment and delivery directly with the seller
4. The transaction happens between you and the seller — outside the platform

**What you get:**
- Ability to negotiate or ask questions before committing
- Seller profiles and reviews to help you decide

**What you do not get:**
- No platform payment processing
- No shipping integration
- No payment protection or dispute resolution through us

:::plain-terms
Contact Seller is like a classifieds board. We help you find the game and connect with the seller, but the actual transaction is between you two. Be careful and use common sense — we cannot help if something goes wrong with payment or delivery you arranged yourself.
:::

## 4. Before You Buy

### 4.1 Check the Listing Carefully

Since you cannot return items just because you changed your mind, check these before purchasing:

| Check | Why It Matters |
|-------|----------------|
| **Condition rating** | Like New, Very Good, Good, or Acceptable |
| **Photos** | Look for wear, damage, missing pieces |
| **Description** | Read for disclosed defects or missing components |
| **Edition and language** | Make sure it is the version you want |
| **Seller reviews** | See what other buyers experienced |

[See our condition guide](/help?section=grading)

### 4.2 Ask Questions First

If anything is unclear, **message the seller before buying**. Good questions to ask:

- "Are all components included?"
- "Is there any damage not shown in photos?"
- "What language is the rulebook/cards?"
- "Has it been played, and how many times approximately?"

Sellers are expected to respond honestly. If a seller is unresponsive or evasive, consider that a warning sign.

### 4.3 Review Seller Profiles

Check the seller's:
- **Rating** — Overall score from previous buyers
- **Reviews** — Read what others said about their experience
- **Number of sales** — More sales usually means more reliable

A seller with no reviews is not necessarily bad — everyone starts somewhere. But be extra careful with high-value purchases from new sellers.

## 5. Payment Protection (Instant Buy)

### 5.1 How It Works

When you purchase an Instant Buy listing:

1. **You pay** — Your payment is processed securely via card or bank link
2. **Seller ships** — They have 24 hours to confirm, then 2 business days to ship
3. **You receive** — Collect from your parcel terminal
4. **You check** — Make sure it matches the description
5. **Order completes** — Automatically 2 days after delivery, or when you confirm earlier
6. **Seller receives funds** — Payment is transferred to the seller

:::plain-terms
The seller does not get your money immediately. You have 2 days after delivery to check the game and report any problems. After that, the order completes and the seller is paid.
:::

### 5.2 What This Protects You From

- **Item significantly not as described** — Missing components, wrong edition, undisclosed damage
- **Item not received** — If tracking shows delivered but you did not get it
- **Damaged in transit** — If the game arrives damaged due to shipping

### 5.3 What This Does Not Protect You From

- **Buyer's remorse** — You cannot return because you changed your mind
- **Minor condition differences** — Subjective disagreements about "Very Good" vs "Good"
- **Not liking the game** — This is not a rental service
- **Contact Seller transactions** — Only Instant Buy has payment protection

## 6. If Something Goes Wrong

### 6.1 The 2-Day Rule

For Instant Buy orders, you have **2 days after delivery** to report a problem.

After 2 days:
- The order automatically completes
- The seller receives payment
- You can still contact the seller directly, but we cannot intervene
- Your last resort is a chargeback through your bank

:::plain-terms
Check your game as soon as you pick it up. Do not wait. If you wait more than 2 days, we cannot help — the seller already has the money.
:::

### 6.2 What Qualifies as a Problem

**We can help with:**
- Game is significantly different from the listing (wrong edition, missing major components)
- Damage not shown or described in the listing
- Item damaged during shipping
- Item not received (tracking shows issues)

**We cannot help with:**
- Minor wear consistent with the condition rating
- You changed your mind
- You did not read the description carefully
- Price disputes after purchase

### 6.3 How to Report a Problem

1. Go to your **Orders** page
2. Find the order and click **Report a Problem**
3. Select the issue type
4. Add photos showing the problem
5. Describe what is wrong

We will review your report and the original listing. If your claim is valid, we will process a refund.

### 6.4 After the 2-Day Window

If you miss the 2-day window:

1. **Contact the seller** — They may agree to a return or partial refund
2. **Leave an honest review** — Help other buyers make informed decisions
3. **Chargeback** — As a last resort, contact your bank to dispute the charge (this is between you and your bank; we cannot control the outcome)

## 8. Shipping and Delivery

### 8.1 How Shipping Works (Instant Buy)

All Instant Buy orders ship via parcel terminals:

- **Unisend** terminals
- **Latvijas Pasts** terminals
- **uDrop** terminals

You choose your preferred terminal at checkout. The seller ships to that terminal, and you collect using a PIN code sent to your phone/email.

### 8.2 Delivery Timeline

| Step | Typical Time |
|------|--------------|
| Seller confirms order | Within 24 hours |
| Seller ships | Within 2 business days after confirming |
| Transit time | 1-3 business days (varies by route) |
| Pickup window | Usually 3-7 days after arrival |

### 8.3 Maximum Delivery Time

- **Within the same country** (e.g. LV→LV): parcels arrive at the terminal the next working day after shipment
- **Between Baltic countries** (e.g. LV→LT): parcels arrive within 2-3 working days after shipment

Including the seller confirmation period (up to 24 hours), the maximum total time from purchase to terminal arrival is **2 working days** for domestic and **4 working days** for cross-border orders.

If your order has not arrived within the maximum delivery time, contact us at info@secondturn.games.

### 8.4 Tracking Your Order

Once the seller ships, you will receive tracking information. Check your order page for updates.

### 8.5 If Your Parcel Does Not Arrive

- Check tracking status on your order page
- If tracking shows "delivered" but you did not receive it, report it within 2 days
- If tracking shows stuck/delayed, contact us at info@secondturn.games

## 10. Returns, Exchanges & Refunds

### 10.1 Order Cancellation

- **Before seller ships:** You may request cancellation. Contact us at info@secondturn.games and we will assist.
- **After shipping:** Cancellation is not possible. You may open a dispute after receiving the item if there is a genuine problem.

### 10.2 Product Returns

Since all sellers on Second Turn Games are private individuals (not businesses), the EU 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply to purchases on this platform. Returns are available only if:

- The item is significantly not as described
- The item was damaged in transit
- The item was not received (tracking shows issues)

To initiate a return, report a problem through your Orders page within **2 days of delivery**.

### 10.3 Product Exchange

Product exchanges are not available on Second Turn Games. Each listing is a unique, pre-loved item. If you receive an item that does not match the listing, report a problem within 2 days of delivery for a refund.

### 10.4 Refunds

- Refunds are available until an order is marked as completed (2 days after delivery confirmation)
- Card payments are refunded to the original payment method
- Wallet payments are refunded to your platform wallet
- After order completion, sales are final and no refunds are available through the platform

:::plain-terms
There are no returns just because you changed your mind. But if the game is not what was described, report it within 2 days of delivery and we will help resolve the issue.
:::
