## 5. Game Listings & Transactions

Listings are offered as **Claim this game** (secure payment and shipping handled through the platform) or **Message seller** (users arrange payment and delivery directly). Listings can have a **Fixed price** or be set up as an **Auction**.

### 5.1 Payment Authorization & Deadlines (Instant Buy)
When you use **Claim this game**, Second Turn provides a secure payment process. Because we act as the authorized agent for the seller, **once you pay us successfully, your payment obligation to the seller is completely settled.** To ensure a smooth marketplace, all Claim this game transactions follow this timeline:
1. **Seller Confirmation:** Sellers must confirm the order within **24 hours**.
2. **Shipping:** Sellers must ship via the designated parcel terminal within **2 business days** after confirming.
3. **Buyer Verification:** Buyers have **2 days after delivery** to check the game.
4. **Completion:** The order completes automatically 2 days after delivery, releasing funds to the seller.

### 5.2 Fees and Shipping
Buyers pay the listed price plus a flat-rate shipping fee; sellers pay a platform commission deducted from the item price. For current rates and detailed breakdowns, see our [Payments & Fees guide](/help/wallet).
