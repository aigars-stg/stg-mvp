## 5. Tax Reporting (DAC7)

**Most sellers on Second Turn Games will never be reported to tax authorities.** If you are just clearing out your personal game collection, you will likely stay well below the reporting thresholds.

Under EU Council Directive 2021/514 (DAC7), we are required to report seller information — but only for those who exceed certain activity levels.

<PlainTermsBox>
Sold 15 games for 500 EUR this year? You are nowhere near the thresholds. This rule exists to catch high-volume commercial activity, not someone selling games from their shelf. And even if you are reported, it does not mean you owe more tax — honest taxpayers will not notice any difference.
</PlainTermsBox>

### 5.1 When We Report

We report your information only if you exceed **either** threshold in a calendar year:

| Threshold | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Number of sales | **30 or more** |
| Total proceeds | **€2,000 or more** |

**You must exceed at least one.** If you stay below both, we report nothing about you.

**Examples:**
- 12 sales, €350 total — **Not reported**
- 25 sales, €1,800 total — **Not reported**
- 35 sales, €900 total — **Reported** (over 30 sales)
- 20 sales, €2,500 total — **Reported** (over €2,000)

### 5.2 We Warn You First

You will never be surprised. We notify you when you reach approximately 80% of either threshold:

- **24 sales** — we will alert you and request your TIN
- **€1,600 in proceeds** — same notification

This gives you time to decide whether to continue selling or provide your Tax Identification Number (TIN).

### 5.3 What We Report

If you exceed either threshold, we report annually to the Latvia State Revenue Service (VID):

| Data | Why |
|------|-----|
| Full legal name | Required by directive |
| Primary address | Required by directive |
| Date of birth | Required by directive |
| Tax Identification Number (TIN) | Required by directive |
| Bank account (IBAN) | Required by directive |
| Total proceeds per quarter | Activity reporting |
| Number of sales per quarter | Activity reporting |

This information is automatically shared with tax authorities in your country of residence via EU information exchange.

### 5.4 Your TIN

Your Tax Identification Number in Latvia is your **personas kods** (11-digit personal code on your ID card). For Lithuanian and Estonian sellers, it is your national personal identification number.

- If you **approach the thresholds**, we will request your TIN
- If you **exceed a threshold** without providing your TIN, selling privileges will be suspended and payouts frozen until you provide it
- Providing a false TIN may result in account termination

### 5.5 What This Does Not Change

**DAC7 reporting does not equal new taxes.** Being reported does not mean you owe anything extra. It is just information sharing between EU countries.

If you are selling personal items occasionally (not running a business), you likely have no tax obligation on those sales anyway. Selling a board game you no longer play is not income — it is just getting back some of what you paid.

You are responsible for understanding your own tax situation. If you have questions, consult a tax advisor.

[Learn more about DAC7](/help?section=dac7)

## 11. Disputes

### 11.1 What Is a Dispute?

A dispute occurs when a buyer reports a problem with their order within the 2-day window after delivery.

### 11.2 Dispute Process

If a buyer opens a dispute:

- We review the buyer's claim against the original listing
- You may be asked to provide evidence (photos, communication history)
- We make the final decision on whether a refund is warranted
- If a refund is issued, the amount is deducted from your wallet

<PlainTermsBox>
If a buyer reports a genuine problem (item not as described, missing components, damaged in transit), we may issue them a refund. That is why honest listings and good photos matter — they protect you if a dispute happens.
</PlainTermsBox>

### 11.3 Protecting Yourself from Disputes

To minimize dispute risk:

- **Describe items accurately** — Honest listings reduce "not as described" disputes
- **Use clear photos** — Show the actual item, including any flaws
- **Communicate promptly** — Respond to buyer questions before and after sale
- **Ship on time** — Delays increase dispute likelihood
- **Keep records** — Save tracking information and buyer communications
- **Package securely** — Damaged items lead to disputes

### 11.4 Bank Chargebacks

In rare cases, a buyer may also dispute the charge directly with their bank. If this happens:

- The disputed amount may be held pending resolution
- You may be asked to provide evidence
- The buyer's bank makes the final decision on chargebacks

### 11.5 Excessive Disputes

If you receive excessive disputes, we may:

- Suspend your selling privileges
- Require you to resolve outstanding disputes before continuing
- Terminate your account

### 11.6 Fraudulent Claims

We recognize that some disputes are fraudulent — buyers who receive items but falsely claim otherwise.

**If you believe you are a victim of a fraudulent claim:**

1. Provide evidence through the dispute process
2. Report the buyer to us at **info@secondturn.games**
3. Provide transaction details and evidence

**We will:**

- Investigate reported fraudulent buyers
- Suspend or ban buyers who abuse the dispute system
- Track patterns of suspicious buyer behavior
- Support sellers with documentation when possible
