## For Buyers
Buyers pay the listed game price plus a flat **€2.00 shipping fee**.

* **Zero Service Fees:** There are no hidden costs or buyer service fees at checkout.
* **Payment Methods:** You can pay using Visa/Mastercard, Baltic bank links (via EveryPay), or your existing platform wallet balance.
* **Split Payments:** If you have wallet balance, it applies automatically at checkout. Any remaining amount is charged to your card or bank.

## For Sellers
Sellers pay a flat **10% platform commission** from each completed **Claim this game** sale.

* **Zero Listing Fees:** Creating listings is completely free.
* **Shipping Excluded:** The commission is calculated only on the item price, never on the shipping fee.
* **No Withdrawal Fees:** Transferring money from platform wallet to your bank account is always free.
* **Contact Seller Listings:** No platform fees apply to Message Seller transactions.

## Your Platform Wallet
Your wallet is where your seller earnings land.

* **Pending Balance:** When an order is delivered, funds are held during the 2-day buyer dispute window.
* **Available Balance:** Once the dispute window passes, funds are available to spend on games or withdraw.
* **Withdrawals:** You can withdraw available funds (minimum €5) directly to your IBAN bank account at any time. Withdrawals typically take 1-3 business days. Your IBAN is collected securely on your first withdrawal request.

## Refunds
If an order is refunded, the money goes back to the original payment method:

* **Card/Bank:** Refunds take 3-5 business days to appear on your bank statement.
* **Wallet:** Refunds to your platform wallet are instant.
* **Split payment:** Each portion is refunded to its original source.
