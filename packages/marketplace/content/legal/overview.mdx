## Legal Framework

Second Turn Games operates under:

- **GDPR** (EU 2016/679) - Data protection
- **DAC7** (EU 2021/514) - Platform seller reporting
- **DSA** (Digital Services Act) - Platform transparency
- **Latvian law** - Governing law for all agreements

Disputes are resolved in the courts of Riga, Latvia.
