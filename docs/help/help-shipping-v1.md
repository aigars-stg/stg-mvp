## The Short Version

**No labels. No post office. Just a code and a locker.**

Shipping on Second Turn Games is simple: we give you a code, you pack your game, you go to a parcel terminal, enter the code, and drop it in. The network handles the rest.

This means you can reach buyers across all three countries from a single listing — and ship from whichever terminal is most convenient for you.

### Finding a Terminal

We have over 1,000 terminals across the Baltics from three networks — all accessible through our integrated terminal map.

**Set your favorite terminal:**

You can save a preferred terminal in your account settings. This makes shipping faster — your closest terminal is always one tap away.

1. Go to **Account Settings → Preferred Terminal**
2. Use the map or search to find a terminal near you
3. Select it as your favorite
4. Done — it'll appear first when you're shipping

**For buyers:** Your preferred terminal auto-selects during checkout (you can still change it).

**For sellers:** Your preferred terminal shows as the suggested drop-off point when you get a shipping code.

**Finding terminals on the go:**

The same map and search you see in checkout is available anytime:
- Search by address, city, or postal code
- Filter by terminal network (Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, uDrop)
- See real-time locker availability (where supported)
- Get directions to any terminal

## Step-by-Step: How to Ship

### Step 1: Confirm the Order

When someone buys your game, you'll receive a notification (email and in-app). You have **24 hours** to confirm you can ship it.

**To confirm:**
1. Go to your Seller Dashboard → Orders
2. Find the new order
3. Click "Confirm Order"

If you can't fulfill the order, click "Reject" — the buyer gets a full refund and no penalty applies (occasional rejections are fine; frequent ones may affect your account).

### Step 2: Get Your Shipping Code

After you confirm, we'll provide you with a **shipping code**. This appears:
- In your dashboard (on the order details page)
- In the confirmation email

The code is typically 6-8 characters. Save it or screenshot it — you'll need it at the terminal.

### Step 3: Pack the Game

This is the most important step. A well-packed game arrives safely; a poorly packed game leads to disputes and bad reviews.

[See detailed packing instructions below →](#how-to-pack-a-board-game)

### Step 4: Drop Off at Terminal

1. Go to any terminal in our network (Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, or uDrop)
2. On the terminal screen, select "Send a parcel" or "Enter code"
3. Enter your shipping code
4. A locker door will open
5. Place your package inside
6. Close the door
7. Done!

The terminal will confirm the drop-off and tracking begins automatically.

### Step 5: Track & Complete

You can track the shipment in your dashboard. The buyer receives:
- Notification when you ship
- Notification when the parcel arrives at their terminal
- A PIN code to collect it

Once the buyer picks up the parcel:
- They have **2 days** to report any issues
- After 2 days (or when they confirm receipt), the order completes
- Your payment is released to your balance

### What Happens After 72 Hours?

If the buyer doesn't pick up within 72 hours:
1. Courier moves parcel to depot
2. Buyer receives SMS with redelivery link
3. Buyer has **4 days** to request redelivery to any terminal
4. If no action → parcel returns to sender

## How to Pack a Board Game

Board games are sturdy, but they need proper packing to survive shipping. Here's how to do it right.

### The Golden Rules

1. **The box should not move** inside the shipping package
2. **Components should not move** inside the game box
3. **Corners need protection** — that's where damage happens
4. **Seal everything** — boxes pop open in transit

### Packing Materials You'll Need

| Material | Purpose | Where to get it |
|----------|---------|-----------------|
| **Outer box or mailer** | Protects the game box | Save boxes from online orders |
| **Bubble wrap or paper** | Padding and corner protection | Post office, shipping stores, free from shops |
| **Packing tape** | Seals everything shut | Any supermarket |
| **Rubber bands or bags** | Keeps components together inside | Optional but helpful |

### Step-by-Step Packing

**1. Secure the inside first**

Open the game and make sure components won't shift during transit:
- Bag loose tokens and pieces
- Rubber-band card decks
- If there's empty space in the box, add paper to fill it
- Put a rubber band around the game box (prevents lid from opening)

**2. Wrap the game box**

- Wrap in bubble wrap, focusing on corners
- Or wrap in kraft paper / newspaper as a base layer
- Make sure all corners have some protection

**3. Choose the right outer packaging**

| Game size | Outer packaging |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Small box (card games, small boxes) | Padded mailer or small box |
| Standard box (Catan-sized) | Shipping box with padding |
| Large/heavy box (Gloomhaven-sized) | Sturdy box with lots of padding |

**4. Fill empty space**

The game should fit snugly with no room to shift:
- Crumpled paper
- Bubble wrap
- Air pillows
- Foam sheets

**Shake test:** Pick up the package and shake it. If you hear or feel movement, add more padding.

**5. Seal it properly**

- Tape all seams of the outer box
- Use quality packing tape (not masking tape or regular tape)
- Consider taping the corners for extra strength

### Common Packing Mistakes

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Better approach |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Using the game box as the shipping box | Always use an outer box — game boxes aren't designed for shipping |
| Just putting the game in a plastic bag | No protection = damage |
| Oversized box with no padding | Game bounces around and gets damaged |
| Weak tape or no tape on game box lid | Lid opens, components scatter |
| Ignoring corner protection | Corners always take the hit — wrap them |

### Carrier Requirements

From Unisend's official packaging rules:

- ✓ Parcel must be **securely closed**
- ✓ All flaps must be **sealed with tape**
- ✓ Pack items so they're **protected from damage**
- ✓ Don't load more than the box can handle
- ✗ No products that could stain or damage other parcels
- ✗ No aerosol packaging

## Size & Weight Limits

Each parcel terminal network has its own locker sizes. Most board games fit in M or L compartments regardless of network.

### Unisend

The most detailed size specifications, with 5 compartment sizes:

| Size | Dimensions (H × W × D) | Fits |
|------|------------------------|------|
| **XS** | 8 × 18.5 × 61 cm | Envelopes, very small items |
| **S** | 8 × 35 × 61 cm | Card games, small flat boxes |
| **M** | 17.5 × 35 × 61 cm | Most standard board games |
| **L** | 36.5 × 35 × 61 cm | Large box games, multiple games |
| **XL** | 74.5 × 35 × 61 cm | Oversized games, big bundles |

**Weight limit:** 30 kg
**Storage:** 72 hours, then 4 days for redelivery request
**Coverage:** Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia

### Latvijas Pasts

The largest network in Latvia with 440+ terminals:

| Specification | Limit |
|---------------|-------|
| **Max dimensions** | 38 × 38 × 58 cm |
| **Max weight** | 30 kg |
| **Storage** | Up to **7 days** |
| **Insurance** | Up to €270 standard (€1,138 with additional) |

**Coverage:** Latvia only (but sends to Unisend lockers in Lithuania/Estonia via partnership)

### uDrop

A shared parcel locker network available to multiple couriers:

| Specification | Details |
|---------------|---------|
| **Locker sizes** | XS, S, M, L, XL (similar to Unisend) |
| **Max weight** | 30 kg |
| **Storage** | 72 hours for recipient |
| **If not picked up** | Sender has another 72 hours to collect |
| **Coverage** | Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia, Poland |

**Unique feature:** If neither recipient nor sender picks up, parcel is stored for 2 months before disposal.

### Quick Comparison

### What If My Game Doesn't Fit?

For oversized games (think: coffin-box games like Gloomhaven, Kingdom Death, etc.):

1. **Try a different terminal** — some locations have XL lockers
2. **Contact the buyer** — discuss alternatives before shipping
3. **Consider splitting** — if selling multiple large items to one buyer, ship separately

If a game truly won't fit any terminal, you may need to arrange alternative shipping with the buyer directly (this is rare for most board games).

## Troubleshooting

### The terminal rejected my package

**Possible reasons:**
- Package too large for available lockers → Try a different terminal with larger lockers
- Package too heavy → Check weight limits
- Code already used → Contact support
- Terminal malfunction → Try another terminal nearby

### My code doesn't work

- Double-check you're entering it correctly (watch for O vs 0, I vs 1)
- Make sure you're at a terminal in our network (Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, or uDrop)
- Check your dashboard — has the code expired or been cancelled?
- Contact support if the issue persists

### The buyer says they didn't receive it

1. Check tracking in your dashboard
2. If tracking shows "delivered to terminal" — the buyer needs to check their notifications for the pickup code
3. If tracking shows stuck/delayed — contact support
4. Keep all tracking information for your records

### I need to cancel after shipping

Once the package is in the terminal, you cannot cancel directly. Contact the buyer and support to arrange a return if needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to print a label?

**No.** Our system uses codes, not labels. Just enter your code at the terminal — no printer needed.

### Can I ship internationally?

Currently, we only support shipping within the Baltic region (Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia) via parcel terminals. For buyers outside this area, use Contact Seller listings and arrange shipping directly.

### What if I miss the 2-day shipping deadline?

The buyer may cancel for a full refund, and your account may be flagged. Repeated late shipments can lead to selling restrictions. If you know you'll be late, communicate with the buyer — many will understand if you're upfront about it.

### Can I use my own shipping method?

For Instant Buy orders: **No.** You must use our parcel terminal network — that's how we provide tracking and buyer protection.

For Contact Seller orders: You arrange shipping directly with the buyer however you both agree.

### What if the game is damaged in transit?

If a buyer reports damage:
1. Review the photos they provide
2. If damage is from poor packing, you may be liable
3. If damage is clearly from carrier mishandling, contact support

**Claims window:** You can submit a claim for lost or damaged parcels within **6 months** from the date sent.

Good packing protects you. Take photos of your packed items before shipping if you want documentation.

### Should I insure valuable games?

The standard shipping includes basic coverage. For particularly valuable games (€100+), consider:
- Extra documentation (photos of packing)
- Messaging the buyer to confirm receipt quickly
- Using Contact Seller for very high-value items where you want more control
