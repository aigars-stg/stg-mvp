## 1. Brand Purpose

### Why We Exist

**Second Turn Games exists to make board gaming more accessible in the Baltic region.**

We believe that:

- **Games are meant to be played.** A €50 game sitting unplayed on a shelf serves no one. When it moves to someone who'll play it, everyone wins.

- **Price shouldn't gatekeep the hobby.** New board games are expensive. A thriving secondhand market makes the hobby accessible to students, families, and anyone who can't justify €50 experiments.

- **Local communities matter.** Global platforms ignore small markets. The Baltics deserve infrastructure built for us, by us — not scraped together from international afterthoughts.

- **Trust enables trade.** Without trust, secondhand markets don't work. Our job is to build the trust infrastructure that lets strangers trade confidently.

### The Problem We're Solving

Board gaming in the Baltics faces a structural problem:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                 │
│   SELLERS                         BUYERS                        │
│   "I have 50 games               "I want to try new games      │
│   I never play"                   but can't afford €50 each"   │
│                                                                 │
│         ↓                              ↓                        │
│                                                                 │
│              NO TRUSTED LOCAL MARKETPLACE                       │
│                                                                 │
│   • General platforms don't understand games                    │
│   • Facebook groups have no payment protection                  │
│   • International shipping costs more than the game             │
│   • No way to verify editions or condition                      │
│                                                                 │
│         ↓                              ↓                        │
│                                                                 │
│   Games sit on shelves        Hobby stays expensive             │
│   Collectors frustrated       Newcomers priced out              │
│                                                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**We exist to fix this.** Not by building a global empire, but by building the right solution for our region.

### Purpose Statement

> **We make board gaming accessible to everyone in the Baltics by creating the trusted local marketplace that connects games with players.**

## 2. Brand Vision

### Where We're Going (5-Year Horizon)

**Vision Statement:**

> **Second Turn Games will be the center of board gaming culture in the Baltics — not just a marketplace, but the home for our community.**

### What This Means

We're not building a "Baltic Vinted for games." We're building the **hub of Baltic board gaming** — starting with a marketplace and expanding into adjacent experiences that strengthen the community.

### Vision Elements

#### Year 1-2: Marketplace Foundation
- Trusted marketplace operating across all three Baltic countries
- Strong community of active buyers and sellers
- Recognized brand among Baltic board gamers
- Sustainable transaction-based business model

#### Year 3-4: Ecosystem Expansion
- Additional features beyond basic marketplace (auctions, trades, ISO matching)
- Premium tools for power users (bulk listing, collection management)
- Community features (user profiles, reputation, social connections)
- Content and resources (pricing guides, condition standards, how-tos)

#### Year 5+: Community Hub
- Physical presence possibilities (annual convention, pop-up events)
- Partnerships with game cafes, publishers, designers
- Platform for Baltic gaming culture (local designers, publishers, events)
- The default answer to "where do Baltic gamers go?"

### Geographic Scope

**We are intentionally Baltic-only.** This is a strategic choice, not a limitation.

| Approach | Why |
|----------|-----|
| **Deep, not wide** | Better to be essential to 100,000 Baltic gamers than irrelevant to millions globally |
| **Regional expertise** | We understand Baltic logistics, languages, culture, and regulations |
| **Sustainable economics** | VAT complexity makes EU-wide expansion costly; regional focus is profitable |
| **Community authenticity** | "Built in Riga for the Baltics" is a genuine story, not marketing |

**The vision is ecosystem expansion (features, experiences, community) — not geographic expansion.**

## 4. Brand Promise

### The One Guarantee

**Brand Promise:**

> **Every transaction on Second Turn Games is protected, verified, and simple.**

This promise has three pillars:

| Pillar | What It Means | How We Deliver |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| **Protected** | Your money and goods are safe | Buyer protection, secure payments, dispute resolution |
| **Verified** | You know what you're getting | BGG integration, condition grades, required photos |
| **Simple** | No hassle, no complexity | One-click shipping, integrated payments, clear process |

### Promise Implications

If we ever face a decision where these conflict, the priority order is:

1. **Protected** — Safety comes first. We never compromise trust.
2. **Verified** — Accuracy second. Better slow and right than fast and wrong.
3. **Simple** — Convenience third. We won't sacrifice safety or accuracy for ease.

## 5. Brand Values

### Our Core Values

Five values guide every decision at Second Turn Games.

---

### 1. Community First

**Definition:** We serve the Baltic board gaming community, not abstract "users" or "customers." Our success is measured by community health, not just transactions.

**What this means in practice:**

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Build features the community asks for | Build features that extract maximum revenue |
| Price fairly so the market works | Charge whatever the market will bear |
| Support community events and spaces | Operate as a detached transaction platform |
| Make decisions that help the ecosystem long-term | Optimize for short-term metrics at community expense |

**Decision test:** *"Does this make the Baltic board gaming community stronger?"*

---

### 2. Trust Through Transparency

**Definition:** We build trust by being honest, clear, and open — about fees, about problems, about what we are and aren't.

**What this means in practice:**

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Show all fees upfront before checkout | Hide fees until the last step |
| Acknowledge problems and mistakes publicly | Pretend everything is fine |
| Explain our decisions and reasoning | Make opaque policy changes |
| Be honest about what we don't know | Fake confidence |

**Decision test:** *"Would we be comfortable if users saw the full reasoning behind this decision?"*

---

### 3. Local Pride

**Definition:** We're built in the Baltics, for the Baltics. We understand our region, speak its languages, and are proud to serve it — not as a stepping stone to something bigger, but as our purpose.

**What this means in practice:**

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Support all three Baltic languages properly | Treat non-English as afterthought |
| Understand regional logistics, culture, habits | Apply generic "European" assumptions |
| Partner with local businesses and communities | Chase global partnerships that ignore local reality |
| Celebrate Baltic gaming culture | Present as discount version of bigger markets |

**Decision test:** *"Does this reflect pride in our region, or treat it as a compromise?"*

---

### 4. Games Are Meant to Be Played

**Definition:** Our core philosophy is that games fulfill their purpose when played. Hoarding helps no one. Trading helps everyone.

**What this means in practice:**

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Celebrate sales as wins for both parties | Frame selling as "getting rid of" |
| Make it easy to let go of unplayed games | Create friction that encourages hoarding |
| Help buyers find games they'll actually play | Optimize only for transaction volume |
| Support the circular economy | Contribute to waste or overconsumption |

**Decision test:** *"Does this help more games get played?"*

---

### 5. Sustainable by Design

**Definition:** We build for the long term. Sustainable unit economics, sustainable workload, sustainable community relationships. No growth hacking that mortgages the future.

**What this means in practice:**

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Charge enough to operate sustainably | Subsidize transactions to inflate metrics |
| Grow at a pace we can support well | Scale faster than quality allows |
| Build features that last | Ship half-finished features to hit deadlines |
| Protect team wellbeing | Burn out chasing growth |

**Decision test:** *"Can we sustain this for 10 years?"*

---

### Values Hierarchy

When values conflict, prioritize in this order:

1. **Community First** — The community is why we exist
2. **Trust Through Transparency** — Trust is how we serve them
3. **Sustainable by Design** — Sustainability lets us keep serving them
4. **Games Are Meant to Be Played** — Our core philosophy
5. **Local Pride** — Our identity and differentiator

### Where We Play

| Dimension | Our Position |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Category** | Board games only — 100% specialized |
| **Geography** | Baltic region only — Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia |
| **Transaction type** | C2C peer-to-peer (may add professional sellers later) |
| **Price segment** | All price points (€5 party games to €200 collector editions) |
| **User segment** | Hobbyists (collectors to casual), not professional resellers |

### Where We Don't Play

| We Don't | Why Not |
|----------|---------|
| Other categories (video games, puzzles) | Dilutes focus and expertise |
| Other geographies (EU-wide, global) | VAT complexity, loses local authenticity |
| B2B wholesale | Different business, different trust model |
| New/retail games | Competes with game shops, not our mission |
| Pure auction model | Adds complexity, may not fit community |

### Positioning Statement

> **For** board game enthusiasts in Latvia, Lithuania, and Estonia
> **Who** want to buy or sell pre-owned games safely and simply
> **Second Turn Games is** the dedicated regional marketplace
> **That** offers verified listings, protected transactions, and integrated Baltic shipping
> **Unlike** general marketplaces or international platforms
> **We** specialize exclusively in board games with deep BGG integration and genuine local focus.

## 7. Competitive Strategy

### How We Win

We don't win by being bigger. We win by being **better for our specific users in our specific market.**

#### Our Competitive Advantages

| Advantage | Why It's Defensible |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **BGG Integration** | Requires technical investment; adds real value to every listing |
| **Baltic Shipping** | Regional logistics partnerships; not worth replicating for small market |
| **Community Trust** | Built over time through consistent behavior; can't be bought |
| **Local Expertise** | Native understanding of languages, culture, regulations |
| **Specialization** | Depth of features that generalists won't build for a niche |

#### Competitive Responses

| If Competitor... | Our Response |
|------------------|--------------|
| **Vinted adds "board games" category** | Double down on specialization; they can't match BGG depth, condition grades, or community expertise |
| **Facebook improves Marketplace** | Emphasize payment protection and shipping; FB won't solve trust |
| **BGG improves European shipping** | Emphasize local speed and cost; we're still faster and cheaper regionally |
| **New Baltic competitor launches** | Win on community, trust, and features; first-mover advantage in relationships |

### Strategic Moat

Our moat deepens over time through:

1. **Network effects** — More sellers attract more buyers, attract more sellers
2. **Data accumulation** — Pricing history, user behavior, market insights
3. **Community relationships** — Trust built through consistent service
4. **Feature depth** — Specialized tools that generalists won't replicate

## 8. Growth Philosophy

### How We Think About Growth

**Philosophy:** Grow sustainably by deepening value, not by chasing scale.

### Growth Priorities (Ordered)

1. **Retention over acquisition** — Better to have 1,000 active users than 10,000 registered users who left
2. **Quality over quantity** — One successful transaction builds more trust than ten abandoned carts
3. **Depth over breadth** — Better features for existing users before new user types
4. **Organic over paid** — Word of mouth from happy users beats advertising

### Expansion Framework

When considering expansion (features, markets, categories):

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    EXPANSION DECISION FRAMEWORK                  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                 │
│  1. Does it serve our existing community better?                │
│     YES → Consider it                                           │
│     NO  → Why are we doing this?                                │
│                                                                 │
│  2. Can we do it excellently, not just adequately?              │
│     YES → Continue evaluation                                   │
│     NO  → Wait until we can                                     │
│                                                                 │
│  3. Is it sustainable (economically, operationally)?            │
│     YES → Continue evaluation                                   │
│     NO  → Find a sustainable version or pass                    │
│                                                                 │
│  4. Does it fit our values and identity?                        │
│     YES → Proceed                                               │
│     NO  → Pass, even if profitable                              │
│                                                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Evolution Roadmap (Illustrative)

| Phase | Focus | Examples |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Foundation** | Core marketplace | Listings, payments, shipping |
| **Depth** | Power user features | Bulk tools, ISO, alerts, price history |
| **Breadth** | Adjacent experiences | Auctions, trades, collection tools |
| **Community** | Physical + digital | Events, meetups, convention presence |

## 9. Strategic Guardrails

### What We'll Never Do

These are lines we won't cross, even if financially attractive.

#### Hard No's

| Guardrail | Reasoning |
|-----------|-----------|
| **We won't sell user data** | Trust is our foundation; data exploitation destroys it |
| **We won't hide fees** | Transparency is a core value; dark patterns erode trust |
| **We won't allow counterfeit games** | Integrity of listings is essential; we verify, not enable |
| **We won't spam users** | Respecting attention is part of respecting community |
| **We won't ignore fraud** | Platform safety is non-negotiable |

#### Soft Boundaries

These are current positions that could evolve with careful consideration:

| Current Position | Condition for Change |
|------------------|---------------------|
| **Baltic-only geography** | Would require sustainable VAT solution and genuine local value |
| **Board games only** | Would require deep expertise and community demand (not just revenue) |
| **C2C focus** | Professional sellers could be added if they meet community standards |
| **No VC funding** | Could change if terms preserved independence and values |

### The "Airport Test"

For any decision, ask: *"If this became a news headline, would we be proud or embarrassed?"*

If embarrassed → Don't do it, regardless of business case.

## 10. Brand Architecture

### Current Structure

Second Turn Games currently operates as a **single brand** with no sub-brands.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                 │
│                      SECOND TURN GAMES                          │
│                                                                 │
│   ┌─────────────┐  ┌─────────────┐  ┌─────────────┐            │
│   │ Marketplace │  │   ISO       │  │   Seller    │            │
│   │  (Core)     │  │  (Feature)  │  │   Tools     │            │
│   └─────────────┘  └─────────────┘  └─────────────┘            │
│                                                                 │
│   All features live under the STG brand umbrella                │
│                                                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Future Possibilities

If we expand into adjacent experiences, we'll need to decide on brand architecture:

| Scenario | Options |
|----------|---------|
| **Game convention** | "Second Turn Con" (sub-brand) or "STG presents: [Event Name]" |
| **Physical cafe/space** | Partner branding or "Second Turn [City]" |
| **Publisher tools** | Feature within STG or separate "STG for Publishers" |
| **Premium tier** | "Second Turn Pro" or simply premium features within STG |

**Principle:** Default to unified brand unless there's a clear reason for separation. Simplicity builds recognition.

## 11. Strategic Decision Framework

### For Any Strategic Decision

Use this framework when facing choices that affect brand, positioning, or strategy.

#### Step 1: Purpose Check

> *"Does this help make board gaming more accessible in the Baltics?"*

- **Yes** → Continue to Step 2
- **No** → Why are we considering it?

#### Step 2: Values Alignment

> *"Does this align with our five values?"*

| Value | Alignment Check |
|-------|-----------------|
| Community First | Does this serve the community or extract from it? |
| Trust Through Transparency | Can we be fully open about this? |
| Local Pride | Does this reflect genuine regional commitment? |
| Games Are Meant to Be Played | Does this help games find players? |
| Sustainable by Design | Can we do this well for years, not months? |

- **All aligned** → Continue to Step 3
- **Conflicts** → Resolve or reconsider

#### Step 3: Strategic Fit

> *"Does this fit where we play?"*

- Within our category (board games)?
- Within our geography (Baltics)?
- Within our user segment (hobbyists)?

- **Yes to all** → Continue to Step 4
- **No to any** → Strong justification required

#### Step 4: Capability Check

> *"Can we do this excellently?"*

- Do we have the skills?
- Do we have the resources?
- Do we have the time to do it right?

- **Yes** → Continue to Step 5
- **Not yet** → Defer until we can

#### Step 5: Sustainability Check

> *"Can we sustain this?"*

- Economically viable?
- Operationally manageable?
- Aligned with long-term vision?

- **Yes** → Proceed
- **No** → Find sustainable version or pass
