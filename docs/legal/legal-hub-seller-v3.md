## 3. Listing Types

Second Turn Games offers two ways to sell, with different levels of service:

### 3.1 Instant Buy — Our Full Service

With Instant Buy, you get the complete Second Turn Games experience:

- **Secure payments** — Payment is processed and you receive funds after delivery is confirmed
- **Baltic-wide shipping** — Reach buyers across Latvia, Lithuania, and Estonia via Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, and uDrop parcel terminals
- **Buyer protection** — Buyers have a 2-day window to report issues
- **Simple shipping** — We provide a code; you enter it at the terminal and load your package
- **No chasing payments** — Everything is handled automatically

**Requirements for Instant Buy:**
- Complete Stripe Connect onboarding (identity verification)
- Be located in Latvia (Estonian and Lithuanian sellers coming soon)

### 3.2 Contact Seller — Basic Listings

Contact Seller is a simpler option — think of it like Facebook Marketplace, but with more structure:

- Buyers message you to discuss price and details
- You arrange payment and delivery directly with the buyer
- No platform payment processing or shipping integration

**When to use Contact Seller:**
- You prefer to arrange your own payment/delivery
- You're located in Estonia or Lithuania (Instant Buy coming soon)
- You want to negotiate price before committing

**No Stripe onboarding required** for Contact Seller listings.

Both Instant Buy and Contact Seller listings can use either pricing format:

**Fixed Price** — You set a price, buyers purchase (or contact you) at that price.

**Auction** — Buyers bid, and the highest bidder wins. The listing shows whether it's an Instant Buy or Contact Seller auction, so buyers know what to expect before bidding.

## 5. Tax Reporting (DAC7)

**Most sellers on Second Turn Games will never be reported to tax authorities.** If you're just clearing out your personal game collection, you'll likely stay well below the reporting thresholds.

Under EU Council Directive 2021/514 (DAC7), we're required to report seller information — but only for those who exceed certain activity levels.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📋 **In plain terms**                                           │
│                                                                 │
│ Sold 15 games for €500 this year? You're nowhere near the       │
│ thresholds. This rule exists to catch high-volume commercial    │
│ activity, not someone selling games from their shelf. And even  │
│ if you are reported, it doesn't mean you owe more tax — honest  │
│ taxpayers won't notice any difference.                          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

### 5.1 When We Report

We report your information only if you exceed **either** threshold in a calendar year:

| Threshold | Amount | At €30 avg. sale |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Number of sales | **30 or more** | 30 games |
| Total proceeds | **€2,000 or more** | ~67 games |

**You must exceed at least one.** If you stay below both, we report nothing about you.

**Quick examples:**
- ✅ 12 sales, €350 total → **Not reported**
- ✅ 25 sales, €1,800 total → **Not reported**
- ❌ 35 sales, €900 total → **Reported** (over 30 sales)
- ❌ 20 sales, €2,500 total → **Reported** (over €2,000)

### 5.2 We Warn You First

You'll never be surprised. We notify you when you reach approximately 80% of either threshold:

- **24 sales** — we'll alert you and request your TIN
- **€1,600 in proceeds** — same notification

This gives you time to decide whether to continue selling or provide your Tax Identification Number (TIN).

### 5.3 What We Report

If you exceed either threshold, we report annually to the Latvia State Revenue Service (VID):

| Data | Why |
|------|-----|
| Full legal name | Required by directive |
| Primary address | Required by directive |
| Date of birth | Required by directive |
| Tax Identification Number (TIN) | Required by directive |
| Bank account (IBAN) | Required by directive |
| Total proceeds per quarter | Activity reporting |
| Number of sales per quarter | Activity reporting |

This information is automatically shared with tax authorities in your country of residence via EU information exchange.

### 5.4 Your TIN

Your Tax Identification Number in Latvia is your **personas kods** (11-digit personal code on your ID card). For Lithuanian and Estonian sellers, it's your national personal identification number.

- If you **approach the thresholds**, we'll request your TIN
- If you **exceed a threshold** without providing your TIN, selling privileges will be suspended and payouts frozen until you provide it
- Providing a false TIN may result in account termination

### 5.5 What This Doesn't Change

**DAC7 reporting ≠ new taxes.** Being reported doesn't mean you owe anything extra. It's just information sharing between EU countries.

If you're selling personal items occasionally (not running a business), you likely have no tax obligation on those sales anyway. Selling a board game you no longer play isn't income — it's just getting back some of what you paid.

You're responsible for understanding your own tax situation. If you have questions, consult a tax advisor.

[Learn more about DAC7 →](/help/dac7)

## 11. Chargebacks and Disputes

### 11.1 What Is a Chargeback?

A chargeback occurs when a buyer disputes a transaction with their bank or card issuer, requesting a refund. This bypasses both you and our platform — the bank makes the decision.

### 11.2 Chargeback Liability

**As the seller, you bear the financial risk of chargebacks.**

If a buyer initiates a chargeback:

- The disputed amount will be debited from your Stripe account
- Stripe charges a chargeback fee (typically ~€15)
- You may provide evidence to contest the chargeback
- The buyer's bank makes the final decision (typically 60-90 days)

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📋 **In plain terms**                                           │
│                                                                 │
│ If a buyer tells their bank "I didn't receive this" or "it      │
│ wasn't as described," the bank can take the money back from     │
│ you. That's why honest listings and good photos matter — they   │
│ protect you if a dispute happens.                               │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

### 11.3 Protecting Yourself from Chargebacks

To minimize chargeback risk:

- **Describe items accurately** — Honest listings reduce "not as described" disputes
- **Use clear photos** — Show the actual item, including any flaws
- **Communicate promptly** — Respond to buyer questions before and after sale
- **Ship on time** — Delays increase dispute likelihood
- **Keep records** — Save tracking information and buyer communications
- **Package securely** — Damaged items lead to disputes

### 11.4 Contesting Chargebacks

If you believe a chargeback is invalid, you can contest it through Stripe by providing evidence such as:

- Proof of delivery (tracking showing delivered)
- Photos of item matching listing description
- Communication with buyer
- Proof that buyer received and accepted the item

Stripe will guide you through the dispute process.

### 11.5 Excessive Chargebacks

If you receive excessive chargebacks, we may:

- Suspend your selling privileges
- Require you to resolve outstanding disputes before continuing
- Terminate your account
- Report suspected fraud to authorities

### 11.6 Fraudulent Chargebacks

We recognize that some chargebacks are fraudulent — buyers who receive items but falsely claim otherwise.

**If you believe you're a victim of chargeback fraud:**

1. Contest the chargeback through Stripe with evidence
2. Report the buyer to us at **info@secondturn.games**
3. Provide transaction details and evidence

**We will:**

- Investigate reported fraudulent buyers
- Suspend or ban buyers who abuse the chargeback system
- Track patterns of suspicious buyer behavior
- Support sellers with documentation when possible

We cannot guarantee chargeback outcomes (banks make final decisions), but we take action against buyers who systematically abuse the system.
