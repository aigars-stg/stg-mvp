## Quick Answers

### Who can use Second Turn Games?

Anyone 16 or older in Latvia, Lithuania, or Estonia can browse and buy. To sell, you must be at least 18.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📋 **In plain terms**                                           │
│                                                                 │
│ The 18+ requirement for selling comes from Stripe, our payment  │
│ processor. They need to verify your identity and can only do    │
│ that for adults.                                                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

---

### How do listings work?

Sellers can choose how they want to sell:

**Instant Buy** — Set a fixed price. Buyers purchase immediately and you ship the game. Simple and quick.

**Contact Seller** — Buyers message you first to discuss price or ask questions. You arrange payment and delivery directly. Good for negotiation or when you're flexible on price.

Both listing types are free to create. You can have multiple active listings at once.

---

### What does it cost?

**Sellers pay nothing to list or sell.** No listing fees, no commission.

**Buyers pay a service fee:** 6% + €0.50 per order, plus €2.00 for shipping via parcel locker.

**Example — a €20 game:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Game price | €20.00 |
| Service fee (6% + €0.50) | €1.70 |
| Shipping | €2.00 |
| **Buyer pays** | **€23.70** |
| **Seller receives** | **€20.00** |

**Payouts to your bank:** When you withdraw your balance, Stripe (our payment processor) charges a small fee for the bank transfer. This fee varies by country and is shown before you confirm the withdrawal.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📋 **In plain terms**                                           │
│                                                                 │
│ Buyers cover all the platform costs. As a seller, what you set  │
│ as your price is what you get — minus a small bank transfer fee │
│ when you withdraw to your account.                              │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

### How are payments protected?

Payment is processed when you buy, and the seller receives funds after the order completes. Here's how it works:

1. **You pay** → Payment is processed by Stripe
2. **Seller ships** → Within 2 business days
3. **You receive** → Check the game matches the description
4. **Order completes** → After 2 days, or when you confirm earlier
5. **Seller gets paid** → Funds are transferred to their balance

If something's wrong, you have **2 days after delivery** to open a dispute.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📋 **In plain terms**                                           │
│                                                                 │
│ The seller doesn't get your money immediately. You have 2 days  │
│ after delivery to check the game and report any problems.       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

[More about buyer protection →](/legal?section=buyer)

### Is this like buying from a shop?

No. All sellers on Second Turn Games are private individuals, not businesses.

**This means:**
- EU consumer protection laws (like the 14-day return right) don't apply
- There's no 2-year legal guarantee
- You're buying from another person, not a company

We provide the platform and payment protection, but we're not a party to your transaction. Think of us as the trusted venue where you meet, not the seller.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📋 **In plain terms**                                           │
│                                                                 │
│ This is more like buying at a flea market than from a shop.     │
│ Check the photos carefully, ask questions, and read the         │
│ condition description before you buy.                           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

### Do I need to worry about tax reporting?

**Most sellers won't be affected.** If you're just clearing out your game shelf occasionally, you'll likely stay well below the reporting thresholds.

EU rules (DAC7) require us to report seller information to tax authorities only if you exceed **either** threshold in a calendar year:
- **30 or more sales**, OR
- **€2,000 or more** in total proceeds

If you stay below **both** thresholds, we don't report anything about you.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📋 **In plain terms**                                           │
│                                                                 │
│ Sold 12 games for €350? Not reported. Sold 25 games for €1,800? │
│ Not reported. But 35 games for €900? Reported (over 30 sales).  │
│ And if you already pay taxes on your income, this changes       │
│ nothing for you — it's just information sharing.                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

We'll warn you when you're approaching either threshold (~80%) so you're never surprised.

[Learn more about DAC7 →](/help/dac7)

### What data do you collect?

The short version:
- **Account info**: Name, email, country
- **Sellers**: Identity details (required by law for tax reporting)
- **Transactions**: Order history, messages, reviews
- **Technical**: IP address, device info (for security)

We never sell your data. We share it only with services that help us run the platform (payments, shipping, email) and when required by law.
