## 1. Our Fee Philosophy

Second Turn Games uses a **buyer-pays model**. Sellers receive their full listing price — buyers pay a small service fee on top.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📋 **In plain terms**                                           │
│                                                                 │
│ If you list a game for €25, you get €25 when it sells. The      │
│ buyer pays the platform fees separately. We think this is       │
│ fairer — you know exactly what you'll receive.                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

| Fee | Amount |
|-----|--------|
| Service fee | **6% + €0.50** |

These fees are added to the listing price at checkout.

No platform fees apply. Any costs depend on how you and the seller arrange payment and delivery.

## 4. Fee Breakdown

### 4.1 Service Fee (6% + €0.50)

The service fee covers:
- **Secure payment processing** — Stripe handles your card payment safely
- **Buyer protection** — Your payment is held until delivery is confirmed
- **Platform operation** — Hosting, development, customer support
- **Fraud prevention** — We monitor for scams and bad actors

The fee has two parts:
- **6% of listing price** — Scales with the value of your purchase
- **€0.50 fixed fee** — Covers base transaction costs

### 4.2 Shipping (€2.00)

The flat €2.00 shipping fee covers delivery via parcel terminal anywhere in Latvia, Lithuania, or Estonia.

**What's included:**
- Terminal-to-terminal delivery (Unisend, Latvijas Pasts, or uDrop)
- Tracking
- Insurance for standard-value items

**Same price everywhere** — Whether you're shipping across town or across the Baltics, it's €2.00.

## 5. Seller Payouts

### 5.1 When You Get Paid

After an Instant Buy order:

1. Buyer purchases → Payment processed
2. You confirm and ship
3. Buyer receives the game
4. Order completes (2 days after delivery or buyer confirms earlier)
5. Funds added to your seller balance
6. **Auto-payout** when your balance reaches your threshold

### 5.2 Auto-Payouts (Free)

We automatically transfer your balance to your bank account when it reaches your payout threshold. **No fees** — we cover the transfer costs.

| Setting | Default | Options |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Payout threshold | €20 | €10, €20, €50, €100 |
| Payout schedule | Weekly | Every Monday for balances above threshold |

**Example:** Your threshold is €20. You sell games for €8, €15, and €12. When your balance hits €35, we automatically send €35 to your bank on the next payout day.

### 5.3 Manual Payouts (Also Free)

Don't want to wait? Request a payout anytime from your Seller Dashboard.

- **Minimum manual payout:** €5.00
- **No fees** — you receive the full amount
- **Processing time:** 2-7 business days (depends on your bank)

### 5.4 Small Balances

If your balance is below €5 and you want to close your account, contact us — we'll transfer any remaining balance regardless of the minimum.

Dormant balances (no activity for 6+ months) are automatically paid out to protect your funds.

## 6. Auctions

Auction fees work the same as fixed-price listings:

- **Instant Buy auction** — Buyer pays 6% + €0.50 service fee + €2.00 shipping on the winning bid
- **Contact Seller auction** — No platform fees; arrange payment directly with seller

## 8. Fee Changes

We may adjust fees in the future. If we do:

- We'll announce changes **at least 30 days** in advance
- Existing listings won't be affected until they're edited or relisted
- You'll have time to remove listings if you disagree with new fees
