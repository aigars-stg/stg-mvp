# STG Launch Marketing Strategy

**Version 1.0 — February 2026**

## Context

Second Turn Games is a Nordic-minimalist peer-to-peer board game marketplace for the Baltic region (Latvia first, then Lithuania and Estonia). Launch is targeted for April–May 2026. The marketplace needs listings to feel alive before attracting buyers — a supply-first strategy.

**Constraints:** Under €500 budget (organic-first), managed by founder via Instagram, Facebook, and email/newsletter. Some existing connections in the Baltic board gaming community.

**Core approach:** Community seeding in existing Baltic board game Facebook groups to recruit "Founding Sellers" pre-launch, then shift to demand generation post-launch.

---

## Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Now → Launch Day, ~6-8 weeks)

**Goal:** 100+ listings live on day one.

### Week 1-2: Foundation
- Create Instagram account and Facebook Page with consistent Nordic-minimal branding
  - Bio: "Every game deserves a second turn. The Baltic marketplace for pre-loved board games. Launching Spring 2026."
- Deploy newsletter signup (PRD exists: `docs/PRD-Newsletter-Feature.md`)
  - Success copy: "You're in. Welcome to the community."
- Identify and join 5-10 active Baltic board game Facebook groups
  - Start participating authentically — answer questions, react to posts, be helpful. No pitching yet.

### Week 3-5: Seed the Supply
- Post organically in groups: share your personal story and the shelf-of-shame problem you're solving. Conversation starter, not an ad.
- DM the 5-10 most active sellers in each group with a personal invite to early access
- Launch "Founding Sellers" program — first 50-100 listers get a profile badge or early-adopter recognition
- Share behind-the-scenes content on Instagram (building the platform, your own game collection, the "why")

### Week 6-8: Pre-Launch Buzz
- Email countdown to subscriber list (T-minus 2 weeks, 1 week, tomorrow)
- Teaser posts on Instagram showing real listings from founding sellers
- Prep launch day assets (screenshots, carousel, announcement copy)

---

## Phase 2: Launch Week

**Goal:** Maximum concentrated signal across all channels in 3-5 days.

### Launch Day Sequence
1. **Email blast** to subscribers: "We're live. The first [X] pre-loved games are already waiting."
2. **Facebook group posts** (all seeded groups, same day): Personal story + marketplace screenshot with real listings
3. **Instagram launch post**: Clean product shot or screen recording. Pin to profile.

### Launch Week Content (Instagram, 1 post/day)
- Day 1: "We're live" announcement
- Day 2: Spotlight a founding seller and their collection
- Day 3: "How it works" carousel (list → buyer pays → ship via parcel locker → get paid)
- Day 4: Specific game listing highlight ("Someone just listed Wingspan for €25")
- Day 5: Social proof ("X games listed in our first week")

### Amplification (zero cost)
- Ask every founding seller to share their listing on their own social media
- Personal social accounts cross-post
- If cafe connections exist, ask them to share or put a flyer at the counter

---

## Phase 3: Post-Launch Growth (Months 1-3)

**Goal:** Shift from supply to demand. Build a content engine that runs consistently.

### Content Engine — Instagram (3x/week, rotating pillars)
1. **Game Spotlights** — "Just listed: [Game] for €[price]." Photo + link. Content that doubles as free advertising.
2. **Seller/Buyer Stories** — Real people, real outcomes. Builds trust.
3. **Board Gaming Culture** — "What's your most-played game this month?" Engagement-first posts.

### Facebook (ongoing)
- Continue genuine participation in Baltic board game groups
- Mention STG only when genuinely helpful (someone posting WTB/WTS)
- Consider starting an STG Facebook group as a community hub

### Email Newsletter (2x/month)
- "New on the shelf" — curated picks from recent listings
- Short community update (milestone, feature, story)
- One CTA per email: browse, list, or share

### Partnerships — Month 2-3 (spend from €500 budget)
- Print flyers/business cards (€50-100) for 1-2 game cafes in Riga
- Propose a "trade-in corner" concept: QR code at cafe counter to list games
- Attend any spring 2026 board game meetups or conventions with printed materials

## Messaging

### For Sellers (primary launch audience)
- **Core:** "Your shelf of shame deserves better. List a game in under 5 minutes."
- **Trust:** "We handle payments and shipping. You confirm the order, drop off the game, get paid."
- **Community:** "Join 50+ founding sellers giving games their second turn."

### For Buyers (post-launch audience)
- **Core:** "Pre-loved games, fair prices, parcel locker delivery."
- **Discovery:** "From sold-out Kickstarters to forgotten classics — find your next game night."
- **Trust:** "Every purchase is protected. Pay securely, pick up at your nearest locker."

### Hashtags (Latvian launch)
`#boardgames #galdasspeles #secondturngames #prelovedgames #boardgamegeek #rigagaming #latvija #tabletopgames #shelfofshame #boardgamemarket`

### Copy Rules (from brand voice guide)
- Always "pre-loved", never "used" or "secondhand"
- No exclamation marks — even in social media
- Contractions always (we're, you'll, it's)
- Sentence case everything
- "Sellers" and "buyers", not "users" or "customers"
- Position positively (STG is a better experience), not against competitors

## Technical Prerequisites

- Newsletter feature needs to be built (`docs/PRD-Newsletter-Feature.md`)
- "Founding Seller" badge — minor profile enhancement (simple UI badge, no complex system needed)
- Landing page or pre-launch mode showing signup + teaser content

## What This Strategy Does NOT Include

- Paid influencer campaigns (out of budget)
- PR/press outreach (can add later if organic traction warrants it)
- TikTok/YouTube (not in manageable channel set)
- BGG forum outreach (not selected as active channel, but worth revisiting in Month 3)
