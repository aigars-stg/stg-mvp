## 4. Tax Reporting (DAC7)

### 4.1 Our Reporting Obligations

Under EU Council Directive 2021/514 (DAC7), we are legally required to collect and report certain seller information to tax authorities.

### 4.2 Reporting Thresholds

You will be reported if you exceed **either** threshold in a calendar year:

- **30 or more** completed sales, OR
- **€2,000 EUR or more** in total sales proceeds

### 4.3 Data We Report

If you exceed the thresholds, we report to the Latvia State Revenue Service (VID):

- Full legal name
- Primary address
- Date of birth
- Tax Identification Number (TIN)
- Bank account identifier (IBAN)
- Total sales proceeds per quarter
- Number of transactions per quarter
- Platform fees (if any)

This information is automatically shared with tax authorities in your country of residence.

### 4.4 TIN Collection

- If you **approach the thresholds** (e.g., 24+ sales or €1,600+ EUR proceeds), we will request your TIN
- If you **reach the thresholds** without providing your TIN, your selling privileges will be suspended and **payouts will be frozen** until you provide it
- Providing a false or invalid TIN may result in account termination and reporting to authorities

### 4.5 Your Tax Obligations

DAC7 reporting does not change your existing tax obligations. You are responsible for:

- Understanding and complying with tax laws in your country
- Declaring income from sales as required
- Consulting a tax professional if you have questions

We do not provide tax advice.

## 5. Listing Rules

### 5.1 What You Can Sell

- ✅ Board games, card games, and tabletop games
- ✅ Items you personally own and have the right to sell
- ✅ Authentic, non-counterfeit items
- ✅ Items in the condition you accurately describe

### 5.2 What You Cannot Sell

- ❌ Counterfeit or pirated games

- ❌ Offensive, discriminatory, or illegal content

### 5.3 Listing Requirements

Every listing must include:

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Accurate title** | Correct game name and edition |
| **True condition** | Honest assessment (New, Like New, Very Good, Good, Acceptable) |
| **Complete description** | Note any defects, missing pieces, wear, or damage |
| **Actual photos** | Clear photos of the actual item you're selling (not stock images) |
| **Fair price** | Reasonable pricing (no price gouging) |
| **Shipping options** | Specify available shipping/pickup options |
| **Language/edition** | Clearly state the game's language and edition |

### 5.4 Condition Guidelines

| Condition | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **New** | Sealed, never opened |
| **Like New** | Opened but unplayed or played once, all components present, no visible wear |
| **Very Good** | Light use, all components present, minimal wear |
| **Good** | Regular use, all components present, visible wear but fully playable |
| **Acceptable** | Heavy use, may have minor component issues, significant wear, still playable |

If components are missing or damaged, you **must** disclose this in the description, regardless of condition rating.

## 9. Chargebacks and Disputes

### 9.1 What Is a Chargeback?

A chargeback occurs when a buyer disputes a transaction with their bank or card issuer, requesting a refund. This bypasses both you and our platform.

### 9.2 Chargeback Liability

> **⚠️ Important: As the seller, you bear the financial risk of chargebacks.**

If a buyer initiates a chargeback:

- The disputed amount will be debited from your Stripe account
- Stripe charges a chargeback fee (typically €15)
- You may provide evidence to contest the chargeback
- The buyer's bank makes the final decision

### 9.3 Protecting Yourself from Chargebacks

To minimize chargeback risk:

- **Describe items accurately** - Honest listings reduce "not as described" disputes
- **Use clear photos** - Show the actual item, including any flaws
- **Communicate promptly** - Respond to buyer questions before and after sale
- **Ship on time** - Delays increase dispute likelihood
- **Keep records** - Save tracking information and buyer communications
- **Package securely** - Damaged items lead to disputes

### 9.4 Contesting Chargebacks

If you believe a chargeback is invalid, you can contest it through Stripe by providing evidence such as:

- Proof of delivery (tracking showing delivered)
- Photos of item matching listing description
- Communication with buyer
- Proof that buyer received and accepted the item

Stripe will guide you through the dispute process. The buyer's bank makes the final decision, which may take 60-90 days.

### 9.5 Excessive Chargebacks

If you receive excessive chargebacks, we may:

- Suspend your selling privileges
- Require you to resolve outstanding disputes before continuing
- Terminate your account
- Report suspected fraud to authorities

### 9.6 Fraudulent Chargebacks

We recognize that some chargebacks are fraudulent - buyers who receive items but falsely claim otherwise. 

**If you believe you're a victim of chargeback fraud:**

1. Contest the chargeback through Stripe with evidence (tracking, photos, messages)
2. Report the buyer to us at **info@secondturn.games**
3. Provide transaction details and evidence

**We will:**
- Investigate reported fraudulent buyers
- Suspend or ban buyers who abuse the chargeback system
- Track patterns of suspicious buyer behavior
- Support sellers with documentation when possible

We cannot guarantee chargeback outcomes (banks make final decisions), but we will take action against buyers who systematically abuse the system.
